# Meta Housing Rules for Real Estate Ads

Used by `SKILL.md` for every job. Real estate ads on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) run under the Housing Special Ad Category. The rules change, so every ad pack ends with: "Check Meta's current policy before you launch." Drafts only. Never connect to an ad account, never launch, and never state a policy detail as settled fact beyond the limits below.

## The limits, in every campaign
- Declare the **Housing** Special Ad Category. Rentals are Housing too. Commercial real estate is unclear, so treat it as Housing unless the member checks Meta's current policy and confirms it does not apply.
- **No targeting by age.** Age stays at its widest range.
- **No targeting by gender.** Gender stays on all.
- **No targeting by ZIP or postal code.**
- **A location radius of at least about 15 miles** (15 miles minimum) around the area served, or a broader area such as a city or county.
- **No lookalike audiences** and no targeting built on age, gender, ZIP, or any other protected trait. Meta may offer broader replacement audiences. Use what Meta allows for the category.
- Let the creative and the offer reach the right people, not narrow targeting.

## Custom audiences from the member's own contacts (optional)
- The member may choose to use a customer-list audience built from their own contacts, such as past clients and their sphere, but only if Meta's Housing category permits it at the time they launch. Never state that it is allowed. Say: "Confirm in Ads Manager that a customer-list audience is available for the Housing category before you use one."
- **Never build, segment, or suggest a list by a protected trait** (age, gender, race, religion, national origin, family status, disability, or a guess at any of them) or by an area's makeup. A list is "all past clients" or "all sphere contacts who agreed to receive marketing", never a slice.
- Only contacts who gave the member their information for marketing may be used, and the member follows Meta's customer-list terms and their brokerage's rules. This is a checklist, not legal advice.
- Never upload anything. The skill only explains what the member would choose in Ads Manager.
- Retargeting is limited to people who engaged with the member's own pages, posts, forms, or videos, and the member confirms in Ads Manager what Meta allows.

## Neighborhood ads
Ads cannot be targeted to one neighborhood or ZIP code. The radius covers the area. The creative names the place and shows the place, the market, or the member. Never offer a workaround, and never describe or imply who lives in the place.

## Copy rules
- **Describe the offer, the property, the place, and the amenities.** Never describe or imply who an ad is for by protected class or by who lives somewhere.
- **Personal attributes.** Meta restricts copy that asserts or implies something about the person viewing the ad, such as their situation or finances. Write about the offer ("A free home value update for homeowners in [area]"), not the viewer ("Is your home stuck?", "Your listing expired", "Struggling to sell?"). The member checks Meta's current policy.
- No steering words ("perfect for families", "ideal for young professionals", "safe neighborhood").
- No school ratings, crime, or safety claims as fact.
- No promised outcomes, guaranteed prices, or quoted rates, and no "will appraise" claims.
- Compensation claims: never state a standard rate.
- Every ad carries the short disclosure line (`disclosure_line_short`) or links to a page with `disclosure_line_full`, and follows `brokerage_ad_rules`. Mark each piece "Needs broker approval before posting" when the rules say so.
- Images show homes, places, and the member. Do not stage images to suggest a type of resident. Use only photos the member owns or has permission to use.
- The landing page headline matches the ad promise and carries the disclosure line.
- Honest proof only: use a proof point only if it is marked public, and never invent a number or a testimonial.

## Special case: communities with an age rule
A 55+ community may state its own age rule as the community's rule if the member supplies it. The copy talks about the community, its amenities, and its rules. Never assume anything about a viewer's age, health, or family.

## Launch checklist (include in every pack)
1. The campaign declares the Housing Special Ad Category.
2. The location is a radius of at least about 15 miles, or a broader area. No ZIP codes.
3. No age or gender targeting, and no lookalikes.
4. If a customer-list audience is used, it is the member's own contacts, not segmented by a protected trait, and the member has confirmed in Ads Manager that it is available for Housing.
5. The ad text and images pass the copy rules and the Fair Housing scan.
6. The ad carries the disclosure line, or links to a page with the full line, and has broker approval if required.
7. The landing page headline matches the ad.
8. The member has checked Meta's current policy for their category.

## Never
Never write an ad that targets or excludes people by a protected trait, never give instructions for getting around a limit, never promise leads or results, and never suggest launching without the member's own review.
