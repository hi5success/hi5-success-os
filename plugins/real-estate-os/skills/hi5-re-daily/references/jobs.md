# Jobs 1 to 7

Used by `SKILL.md`. Every job is read only. Scan first (the connected sources, the profile, and the CRM Map), ask only about what could not be found, and point to what you found. Show the score logic for any ranking in one line per person. Filter notes for protected class details and steering remarks, and say in one line what you left out. Never write, send, or change anything. Drafts appear in the chat. To act on an item, point to /hi5-re-crm.

Look for these in the CRM before asking: the stage and value of each opportunity, the last touch, the open tasks, the transaction fields (contract, closing, inspection or option, appraisal, and financing dates), the tags (client type, source, do-not-disturb), the consent source, and the notes.

---

## Every day

### 1. Morning briefing
Cover today and tomorrow, from the CRM, Gmail, and Calendar. Use short bullets and write "None" for an empty section.
1. **Today's schedule:** each appointment with the time, the place, the client, and a one line prep note (the last CRM note and the open opportunity, what to bring, what to confirm).
2. **Needs a reply today:** emails from clients, lenders, title or escrow, other agents, and the broker that need action. One line each: the ask and a suggested reply angle.
3. **Overdue and due today:** tasks past due (oldest first, with days late) and tasks due today, with the contact.
4. **Deadlines:** deals under contract with an inspection or option, appraisal, financing, or closing date in the next 7 days, and any contract date, offer deadline, or document due in the next 48 hours mentioned in email.
5. **Going cold:** open opportunities with no activity in 10 days (the member can change the number), the top 5 by value.
6. **New leads with no reply** since yesterday.
7. **Can wait:** counts only, with no detail.
8. **Top 3 moves today**, ranked by money at stake and time sensitivity.
Do not change anything.

### 2. Who to call today
1. Scan the CRM for leads (stage, source, last contact, what they want, and the notes after the filter). If the member pasted a list, use that. Ask how much time they have only if they did not say.
2. Rank by likelihood to transact in 90 days (the stage, whether they replied, and the timeline they gave), days since the last contact, estimated value, and how warm the last conversation was. Show the score logic in one line per lead.
3. For the top leads (as many as fit the time, 5 by default) give:
   - why they are on the list, in one line
   - the best channel today (call, text, email, or video). Suggest a text only if the consent check passes. Otherwise suggest a call or an email and say why. Never suggest outreach to someone marked do-not-disturb
   - a text under 300 characters that refers to something specific from the notes and ends with a question
   - an email subject and 3 sentences
   - a call opener (the first 2 sentences) if a call is best
4. For everyone else, a one line "next touch" with a date.
No "just checking in". Every touch must offer something useful (a listing the member has, a fact from the member's own data, an answer, or an invitation).

### 3. End-of-day shutdown
Inputs: what got done, what did not, what new came up, and tomorrow's fixed appointments. If the CRM, Gmail, or the calendar is connected, read the open tasks, today's sent mail, and tomorrow's calendar for promises and appointments, read only.
Deliver, under 150 words:
1. **Open loops:** anyone the member promised something to who is still waiting, each with a one line message that takes under 2 minutes (text only if the consent check passes).
2. **Tomorrow's top 3,** in order, with the first one placed in the member's most focused hour.
3. **One item to delegate, automate, or drop,** and why.
4. **One line on what went well today.**
If the same "not done" item keeps repeating, say so plainly.

## Every week

### 4. Who needs attention this week
Find these, from CRM data only:
1. Hot and warm leads (by tag or stage) not contacted in 7 days or more.
2. Leads who replied and have not heard back.
3. Clients under contract with a deadline this week.
4. Past clients with a home anniversary or birthday this week, only if those fields exist. Use the date only to prompt a card or a note. Never copy a date of birth anywhere.
5. Sphere or past clients with no touch in 90 days or more, up to 10, highest value or most recent closing first.
6. Open tasks due this week, grouped by contact.
Output: start with "This week: X calls, Y texts, Z emails" (texts only for people who pass the consent check). Then one ranked list of at most 15 people: Name, Why now, Last touch, Suggested action (call, text, email, or task), and a one line opener. Then "Can wait": everyone else who matched, names only. Base "why now" only on CRM data. Do not change anything.

### 5. Friday deal rescue review
Read the active pipeline (or the pasted export): client, side, stage, price, key dates, last contact, and next step.
1. **On track:** each deal, the next milestone, its date, and who owns it.
2. **At risk:** deals with no contact for 7 days or more, a missed date, or a stalled stage. For each give the likely reason (from the data), the next action, and a 2 sentence check-in message in the member's voice.
3. **Deadlines:** every inspection or option, appraisal, financing, and closing date in the next 14 days, sorted by date.
4. **Forecast:** what is likely to close this month and next, with a confidence (high, medium, or low) and why, only from the data. Label any assumption.
5. **One big move:** the single action this week that protects the most income.
Use a table for sections 1 to 3. Flag anything that needs the broker, a lender, or an attorney. Drafts only. For a file that needs full management, point to /hi5-re-transaction.

### 6. Week-ahead calendar prep
Read the next 7 days of the calendar.
1. **Day by day:** label each appointment (buyer consult, listing appointment, showing, inspection, closing, team, personal, other), and list what to prepare (the CMA, a buyer packet, the contract, directions, signs), what to send the client beforehand, and whether it needs a confirmation today.
2. **Confirmations:** for each client appointment in the next 48 hours, draft a short confirmation under 60 words with the time, the address, and what to bring. Show them in the chat. Save them as Gmail drafts only with the member's OK for that batch, and never send.
3. **Overload:** flag days with more than 5 appointments (the member can change the number) or under 20 minutes between stops in different places.
4. **Gaps:** find open blocks of 60 minutes or more on weekdays, and suggest which to use for prospecting, follow-up, or content. Do not create events. To block time, show the plan and create events only after the member's OK, through the calendar connection.
5. **Appointment briefs:** offer a one page walk-in-ready brief for any consult: the opening for the first 2 minutes, 6 discovery questions from easy to deeper, the top 3 likely objections with the member's own answers from the Objection Bank, what to bring, the exact ask with a fallback next step, and a follow-up text for within an hour (consent check first). For a listing appointment, point to /hi5-re-listing-appt.
Format: one short block per day, then "Confirmations drafted: X" and "Suggested time blocks: Y".

## Inbox

### 7. Inbox triage and lead rescue
Read the member's Gmail for the last 3 days (the member can change the number), read only.
1. **Count:** the emails reviewed and how many need action.
2. **Buckets:** Reply today (clients, offers, contracts, deadlines, new inquiries), Reply within 24 hours, FYI only, and No action.
3. **Lead rescue:** list every person who looks like a lead or prospect (asked about a home, a valuation, a showing, or working with the member) and did not get a reply. Show the name, what they asked, and the hours since they wrote. Put anyone waiting over 48 hours at the top, marked OVERDUE. Check the CRM so you do not treat an existing client as a new lead.
4. **Drafts:** for each Reply today, Reply within 24 hours, and lead rescue item, write a reply draft in the chat, under 120 words. Lead replies answer their question directly, then offer one clear next step (a call time or a showing slot). No lead reply opens with an apology paragraph. One short acknowledgment of the delay is enough. Save drafts to Gmail only with the member's OK for that batch, and never send.
5. **Cracks:** flag any thread where someone is waiting on the member for a document, a decision, or a call back.
Output most urgent first, one line per email.

## Make it run itself (Cowork routine)
Offer these steps once. The member sets it up. Menus change, so offer screenshot help and never assume menu names.
1. In Claude Cowork, open Scheduled tasks and choose to create a new one.
2. Name it "Hi5 morning briefing".
3. For the instruction, paste: "Run /hi5-re-daily job 1, the morning briefing. Read only. Do not send, save, or change anything. Show the result in the chat."
4. Choose weekdays at the time they want (for example 7:30am).
5. Confirm that Gmail, Calendar, and the CRM connections are on for scheduled runs.
The same steps work for job 4 (weekly, Monday morning) and job 5 (Friday afternoon).
