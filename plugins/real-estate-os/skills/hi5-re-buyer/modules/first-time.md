# Module: First-Time Buyer

Someone buying their first home. They need the process explained without jargon, and a lender early.

## Discovery questions (skip anything the scan answered)
1. What is making now the right time to buy?
2. Have you talked to a lender yet? If not, would you like an introduction to the lenders the member works with?
3. What have you seen so far that you liked or did not like?
4. How are you thinking about the monthly cost and the cash you need up front? (The lender and the buyer supply the numbers. Never calculate them.)
5. What must the home have? What would be nice?
6. What would make you nervous about this process?

## Process notes
- Walk through the steps in the order the member's contract uses, in plain English, with a 5 word explanation for every term.
- Pre-approval comes first. The lender gives the figures. Never state what the buyer can afford.
- Cash needed up front falls into categories (earnest money, inspection, appraisal, closing costs, moving costs). Name the categories and say the lender and the title company provide the figures. Never state amounts or percentages.
- Assistance programs: say the lender and the state or local housing agency know what exists and who qualifies. Never state that a program exists, who qualifies, or an amount.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "I need to save more first."
2. "I'm scared of making a mistake."
3. "Should I wait for rates to drop?" Use job 12. Never predict.
4. "Why do I need an agent?" Describe what the member does. Compensation is set by agreement and is negotiable. Never call it standard.
5. The member's Objection Bank answers for first-time buyers.

## Follow-up notes
Send a short "what happens next" note after every meeting. Keep every message free of jargon. Offer the first-time buyer guide from /hi5-funnel or /hi5-email as a nurture resource.
