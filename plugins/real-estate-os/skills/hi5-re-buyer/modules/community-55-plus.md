# Module: Buyer in a 55+ or Age-Restricted Community

A buyer interested in a community with its own eligibility rules. Eligibility is the community's own process.

## Discovery questions (skip anything the scan answered)
1. Which communities are you considering, and what draws you to them (amenities, the lifestyle features, the maintenance arrangement)?
2. What do you need from the home (single level, a garage, a guest room, a low-maintenance lot, accessibility features)?
3. What is your timeline?
4. Have you read the community's documents, or would you like to go through them together?

## Notes
- Never guess, ask, or screen by anyone's age or family status. Say that the community has its own application and approval process and that its documents state the rules. The member reads what the documents say and does not interpret the law.
- Build a checklist from the community's own documents: the application and approval steps and their timeline, the resale documents, transfer and monthly fees and what they cover, rules for rentals, guests, and pets, the budget and reserves if the documents provide them, and any right of first refusal. Never state fees or rules from memory, only from the documents.
- Property needs are features such as a ground floor, a roll-in shower, or a wide doorway. Record them as features, never as facts about the person.
- Never describe who lives in the community. Describe amenities and facilities by what exists.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "I'm not sure about the monthly fees."
2. "What if I don't get approved?" Say the community decides, and the member helps with the application steps.
3. The member's Objection Bank answers.

## Follow-up notes
Send the application checklist and the document list after the first meeting. Keep the dates from the community's own documents.
