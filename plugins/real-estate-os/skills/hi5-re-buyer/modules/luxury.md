# Module: Luxury Buyer

A buyer at a high price point, where privacy, access, and careful due diligence matter.

## Discovery questions (skip anything the scan answered)
1. What matters most: the property itself, privacy, a specific feature, or timing?
2. How do you want to be represented (confidentiality about who is looking, who attends showings, and how information is shared)?
3. Who else needs to approve or advise (an attorney, a family office, a business manager)?
4. How will you pay, and are there any proof-of-funds steps the sellers or the listing side will ask for?
5. What due diligence do you want (inspections, surveys, systems, permits, and any specialists)?

## Notes
- Comps are often thin. Say so plainly, and use fewer, closer comps the member gives, with reasons. Never state a value.
- Privacy: keep the buyer's name and details out of any message to the listing side unless the buyer approves it, and follow the brokerage's policy on private showings and access.
- Allow a longer due diligence window only if the contract allows it, and ask the member's broker.
- Describe value drivers (land, view, craftsmanship, location) as features of the property, never as who the buyers or neighbors are.
- Private listings and off-market properties: the member follows the MLS and brokerage rules and never states what they allow.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "I want to see it before anyone else does."
2. "Is the price reasonable?" Point to the member's comps and say how thin they are.
3. The member's Objection Bank answers for luxury buyers.

## Follow-up notes
Be discreet and precise. Confirm what may be shared and with whom before sending anything.
