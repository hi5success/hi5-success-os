# Module: Relocation and Remote Buyer

Someone moving from another area, often buying with few or no in-person visits.

## Discovery questions (skip anything the scan answered)
1. When do you need to be in, and is there a start date or a lease end behind it?
2. How many trips can you make, and when?
3. What does the home need to have, and what is it near that matters to you (addresses you name, such as an office or a family member's address)?
4. Will you tour by video, in person, or both?
5. Who is the best contact for signing and for closing day, and what time zone are you in?

## Remote notes
- Put the time zone on every deadline, message, and call.
- Video tours: the member shows the property and the surroundings by features and facts, and never describes who lives nearby. Offer to send a recorded walkthrough for the buyer to review.
- Remote signing and a remote walkthrough: the member confirms with the title company and the broker what is available. Never state what a state allows. See /hi5-re-transaction (the relocation notes) for the closing steps.
- Area research: if the buyer asks what an area is like, give the official sources (the county, the city planning office, the school district's own site, local government data) and ask what the buyer needs in property terms (a commute, a lot size, a quiet street). Never rate or describe areas by the people.
- The sale of the buyer's current home is a separate plan. Point to /hi5-re-listing-appt if the member works that market, and to a trusted agent in that market if not, per the broker's referral rules.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "I don't want to buy without seeing it."
2. "I don't know the area."
3. "Can I count on photos and video?"
4. The member's Objection Bank answers.

## Follow-up notes
Send a weekly update in the buyer's time zone. Keep a single point of contact on closing day.
