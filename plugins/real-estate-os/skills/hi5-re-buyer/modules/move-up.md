# Module: Move-Up or Move-Down Buyer

Someone who owns a home and is buying another, usually with a sale involved.

## Discovery questions (skip anything the scan answered)
1. Do you need to sell before you buy, buy before you sell, or do both together?
2. Is there a date that drives the timing (a lease on a temporary place, a job, a school year you named)?
3. What does the next home need that this one does not?
4. How are you planning to fund the purchase, and have you talked to a lender about how the sale and purchase fit together?
5. What would you do if the sale and the purchase did not line up?

## Sequencing notes
- Lay out the options in plain terms: sell first, buy first, a contingent offer, a sale with a rent-back or a longer closing, or a temporary place. Say each has trade-offs, and say the lender, the member's broker, and an attorney confirm what is possible. Never state what a contract or a lender will allow.
- The sale side is its own plan. Point to /hi5-re-listing-appt (and /hi5-re-listing-launch for the launch) and keep the two timelines on one page so the buyer sees how they fit.
- Never calculate proceeds, a payment, or equity. The buyer and the lender supply those numbers, and a CMA supplies pricing.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "I don't want to sell until I know I have found the next one."
2. "I don't want to carry two mortgages."
3. "My home is worth more than that." Point to the CMA and never state a value.
4. The member's Objection Bank answers.

## Follow-up notes
Give the buyer one timeline sheet that shows both sides. Update it after every step.
