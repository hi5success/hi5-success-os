# Module: Home Seller

The default listing appointment for a house, condo, or townhome.

## Discovery questions (put the most important first)
1. If this home sold on your timeline, what would that make possible for you?
2. Who else is part of the decision, and what will each of you need to see?
3. What happens if it does not sell by your target date?
4. What did you like and not like about the agents you have talked to?
5. What have you updated or repaired, and roughly when?
6. Where are you heading next, and does your timing depend on this sale?

## Pricing notes
- Comps: same type, same area or subdivision, closed in the last 6 months, plus actives and pendings for context.
- Show the price in terms of buyer behavior (showings, days on market), never the agent's opinion of the seller.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "Your fee is too high." Compensation is set by agreement and is negotiable. Explain the value and what is included. Never call a fee standard.
2. "Let's try my price for a couple of weeks first."
3. "Another agent said they'd list higher."
4. "I'm considering a discount broker or selling on my own."
5. "I don't want to do any repairs or prep."
6. "I'd rather wait for a better season or better rates."
7. "An online estimate says my home is worth more."
8. Anything the member adds, and the member's Objection Bank answers for sellers.

## Follow-up notes
Use the member's own words from the appointment. Keep texts to documented consent. Every email ends with the disclosure line.
