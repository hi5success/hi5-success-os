# Module: Seller in a 55+ or Age-Restricted Community

A home in a community that is designated for older residents. The rules come from the community, so this module is about the property, the community's documents, and the seller's plan.

## Discovery questions
1. What is your plan for after the sale: a smaller home, a different community, or something else?
2. What is your timing, and does it depend on where you are going next?
3. What do the community documents say about resale (approval steps, transfer fees, monthly fees, and timelines)?
4. Who else is involved in the decision (a co-owner, a family member helping, an estate representative)?
5. What has been updated or repaired, and roughly when?
6. What do you want to take with you, and what will stay?

## Pricing notes
- Use comps from the same community first, then similar communities, closed in the last 6 months. Say why if you widen.
- Community fees, transfer fees, and approval timelines affect the net and the closing date. Get them from the seller's documents and show them as facts the seller gave.

## Fair Housing for 55+ (always)
- Eligibility is the community's own legal process. The member must not guess, screen, or imply who may buy based on age.
- Describe the community and the home by features and amenities only. Say "this is a [community name] community" only if the member confirms its lawful designation. Never describe or suggest who lives there or who "belongs" there.
- Never assume the seller's age, health, or reasons from their stage of life. Record only what they say about their goals and timeline.
- Confirm the community's rules, and anything about advertising it, with the community and the member's broker or attorney.

## Objections (keep, delete, or add)
1. "The pool of buyers is smaller, so it will take longer." Respond with local data from the member, not assumptions about people.
2. "Your fee is too high." Compensation is set by agreement and is negotiable. Never call it standard.
3. "I want to wait until I find my next home."
4. "I'm not sure I'm ready to leave."
5. "The community fees are keeping buyers away." Use the numbers the member has.
6. The member's Objection Bank answers for 55+ sellers.

## Follow-up notes
Be patient and never rush. Offer a timeline that follows the seller's next move. Every email ends with the disclosure line.
