# Showing Feedback Filter

Used by `/hi5-re-seller-updates` before any showing feedback goes into an update, a price case, or a diagnosis. Showing agents sometimes include comments about the buyers or the neighborhood's people. Those must never be recorded or passed on.

## Keep
Reactions to the home: price, condition, layout, features, finishes, location facts (the road, the lot, the commute), the smell, the light, what the buyer compared it to, interest level, and what held the buyer back about the property.

## Leave out, and say so
Anything about the buyer's or the neighbors' protected characteristics or circumstances, and anything that steers: family size or makeup, age, religion, national origin, language, disability, race, or who "would fit" the area. Also drop remarks that describe the neighborhood's people.

How to handle it:
1. Drop the remark. Keep the property part if there is one. For example, "the family wanted a bigger yard" becomes "wanted a larger yard".
2. Tell the member in one line what you left out and why, for example: "I left out one comment about the buyers' family because it is a protected class detail. I kept the part about the yard."
3. If the remark shows a steering request (for example "the buyer asked what kind of people live here"), do not answer it. Say the member can only describe the property and point to official sources, and suggest the member follow their broker's policy.
4. Never copy the remark into the CRM, the Marketing Hub, or a seller update.

## Showing feedback requests
Ask only about the buyer's reaction to the home: interest level, the one thing that held them back, and whether the price seemed right, high, or low. Never ask about the buyer's personal characteristics or circumstances.
