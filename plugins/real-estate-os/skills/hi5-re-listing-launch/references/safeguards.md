# Listing Safeguards

Used by `/hi5-re-listing-launch` and `/hi5-re-seller-updates`. Read it with the shared guardrails file. Scan first, then ask: only ask about a safeguard if the profile and the saved pages do not already answer it, and save the answer so you never ask again.

## 1. Sharing a sale, a contract, or a client's story
- **Seller permission.** Before a just-sold or under contract post, confirm the seller approved sharing. If they did not, keep it to the property and nothing personal. Use a client quote or a client's name only with their permission.
- **Sale price.** Some states and MLSs do not allow publishing a sold price. Read `sold_price_policy` from the profile. If it is blank, ask once, "Can you publish sold prices in your state and MLS?", and save the answer. If the answer is no or unknown, leave the price and any number that reveals it out of every public piece. Texas, for example, is a non-disclosure state, so the member confirms it.
- **Say "under contract" or "pending" until it closes.** Never say sold before closing.
- Never share the terms of a contract, the buyers' details, or the seller's reasons.
- A client quote is a testimonial, so use it only if the client agreed and it is true.

## 2. Photos, staging, and edits
Photos belong to the member or the seller. If photos were virtually staged or edited, say so in the public copy, and never alter a photo to hide a defect. When the member uploads photos, describe only what is visible about the property, mark anything you cannot confirm `not verified`, and never describe the people in a photo.

## 3. Broker approval and advertising rules
Read `brokerage_ad_rules`. If broker approval, team-name rules, or a license or brokerage name format applies, say so in one line, mark every public piece "Needs broker approval before posting", and follow the format.

## 4. MLS remarks and coming soon
- Remarks length: read `mls_remarks_limit`. If blank, ask once, and save the answer.
- Coming soon marketing before the list date: read `coming_soon_policy`. If blank, ask once, "Does your MLS and broker allow coming soon marketing before list date?", and save the answer. If no or unknown, do not draft teasers.
- Whatever the MLS or broker prohibits in remarks (contact details, brokerage promotion, and the like) stays out. Do not guess the rules.

## 5. Open house sign-ins and follow-up
Follow up only with people who gave contact information and agreed to be contacted. Provide sign-in wording that says why you ask and how you will use it, and that texts need their agreement, for the member's broker to review. Log visitors in the CRM with /hi5-re-crm job 3 after the member's OK, and run the consent check before any text. Never use phrasing that lets visitors choose who moves into the neighborhood.

## 6. Paid ads
Paid listing ads are coming in /hi5-re-ads. Until then /hi5-funnel follows Meta's Housing special ad category. Any ad task in a calendar says: no age, gender, or ZIP targeting, and a radius of about 15 miles or more.

## 7. Posting
Nothing is posted or scheduled by this skill. The member copies the drafts into their own tools. If they want steps for their scheduler, give numbered instructions with screenshot help and never ask for a password.
