# Fair Housing Scan

Used by `/hi5-re-listing-launch` and `/hi5-re-seller-updates`. Run it on every draft before the member sees it. It is a check, not legal advice.

## The rule
Describe the home, the lot, and nearby amenities. Never describe or imply who should live there, who lives nearby, or who a home suits. This applies to every client type and every property type, and to photos (describe what is visible about the property, never the people in a picture).

## What to look for

### People and who the home suits
Words that describe or imply a group: family or families, kids, children, couples, singles, bachelor or bachelorette, retirees, seniors, elderly, young, empty nesters, newlyweds, professionals, and any religion, nationality, or language. Also phrases that say who a home suits: "perfect for", "ideal for", "great for", "suited to", "built for". The only exception is a lawfully designated 55+ or age-restricted community, where the community's own approved wording is used and the member confirms it with the community or their broker.

### Place and people claims
"Safe", "secure", "crime-free", "quiet" used to mean the people, "exclusive" (as in an exclusive community), "good schools", "great schools", "top-rated", religious places used as a draw ("steps from the church"), and any description of who lives in an area.

### Disability and access
Never say or imply who can or cannot use the home. Describe features instead: "step-free entry", "main-floor primary suite", "36-inch doorways", "roll-in shower". Do not use "able-bodied", "handicapped only", or "no wheelchairs".

### Claims
School ratings, crime levels, flood zones, appreciation, investment returns, guaranteed outcomes, and "will appraise". If schools matter, give the assigned school names only if the member provides them, and say to verify with the district.

### Pressure and filler
Banned words and phrases: nestled, dream home, boasts, stunning, gorgeous, must-see, won't last, rare find, pride of ownership, turnkey, cozy, charming, motivated seller, as-is, entertainer's dream, oasis, priced to sell, welcome home. For price changes, write "new price" or "price improvement", never "reduced", "slashed", or "must sell".

### Room and term notes
"Family room" is a room name and is fine. Prefer "primary bedroom" to "master bedroom" and "guest suite" or "second living suite" to "mother-in-law suite".

## How to fix what you find
Replace the phrase with a fact about the property: a feature, a measurement, a distance, a year, or a material. Examples: "perfect for a young family" becomes "four bedrooms, three on one level, and a fenced yard". "Safe neighborhood" becomes the plain facts, such as "a cul-de-sac with a sidewalk and a park 0.3 miles away".

## What to tell the member
Only if something changed, one line per change: what you replaced, and why in plain words. If nothing changed, say "Fair Housing scan: clean." Never lecture.

## Ads and rentals
Any housing ad runs under Meta's Housing special ad category (no age, gender, or ZIP targeting, radius of about 15 miles or more). For rentals, never state a tenant preference, and describe screening only as written criteria applied the same way to everyone. Read `protected_class_jurisdictions` on the Compliance Guardrails page for classes protected where the member works.
