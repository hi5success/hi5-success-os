# Property Types

Used by `/hi5-re-listing-launch` and `/hi5-re-seller-updates`. Load the section that matches the listing. If it is a mix, load the closest and say what you borrowed.

## Home for sale (the default)
- Facts to gather: price, beds, baths, living area, lot, style, year built, top 3 to 5 specific features, updates with years, nearby amenities with distances, and how the home lives (features only).
- Comps and market numbers come only from what the member gives you.

## Rental listing
- Say "available" and give the rent, the lease term, the move-in date, what is included, and the application steps. A "just leased" post replaces just sold. An "open showing" replaces an open house.
- Never state a tenant preference. Describe screening only as written criteria applied the same way to every applicant. Say assistance animals are welcome as the law requires only if the member's attorney or broker approves the line, and describe a pet policy as a policy.
- Rent, deposits, and fees come only from the owner and the member. Deposit and fee limits differ by state and city, so say to confirm them with the broker or attorney, and never state a legal limit.
- Read `protected_class_jurisdictions`, because many places protect more classes, such as source of income.

## Investor or income property
- Facts only: the number of units, current rents and occupancy as the owner provides them, lease terms, and expenses the owner gives. Label every figure with its source.
- Never promise or imply a return. A cap rate or income figure appears only if the member supplies it, labeled "the owner's figure".
- If tenants live there, showings are subject to notice, and tenants' privacy matters. Do not publish photos of occupied units without permission.

## 55+ or age-restricted community
- Use the community's name and its amenities and the home's features. Say "a 55+ community" only if the member confirms its lawful designation, and use the community's own approved wording.
- Never imply who belongs there, and never guess or screen anyone by age. Eligibility is the community's own process.
- Confirm advertising wording with the community and the member's broker.

## Luxury
- Ask the seller what may be public (address, photos, price) before drafting anything. Private or off-market marketing is the seller's choice, and the MLS and broker rules still apply.
- Lead with craftsmanship, land, views, and materials as facts. "Exclusive" is banned.
- Comps may be thin, so say plainly when a comp is not a close match.

## Land, new construction, and commercial
- Facts only, from documents: acreage, frontage, access, utilities, and survey details. Quote zoning or permitted use only from a document, and name the source. Say "buyer to verify".
- New construction: use the builder's materials and plans as the builder states them. Completion dates are the builder's estimates, never promises.
- Commercial: facts from the offering documents, a rent roll the owner provides, and lease terms. Leases and letters of intent need attorney review.
- Treat any advertising as under the Housing special ad category unless the member's broker confirms otherwise.
