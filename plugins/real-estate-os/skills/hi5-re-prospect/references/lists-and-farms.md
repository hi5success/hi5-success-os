# Lists and Farms

Used by `SKILL.md` for jobs 1 to 8. The gate in `../../hi5-re-sphere/references/touch-rules.md` always applies.

## Contact lists the member brings
- **Accepted sources:** the member's own CRM, a list from their own data vendor (for example REDX, Vulcan7, or Landvoice), a list they built, or a list they typed or pasted. The vendor's terms and the member's MLS rules are the member's to follow.
- **Never:** skip trace, search for or fill in a missing phone number, email, or mailing address, collect contact details from a listing site, a social platform, or an MLS, or buy or scrape anything on the member's behalf. If a row has no usable contact, list it as "no contact on the list" and move on.
- **Scrub confirmation.** Before any call script or voicemail for a list, ask once, in one line, pointing to what you found: "This list has 40 numbers and I see no scrub date. Did you scrub them against the National Do-Not-Call Registry, your state list, and your own opt-out list, and when?" Note the date the member gives. A list marked "DNC-scrubbed" by a vendor still needs the member's own confirmation, and the member's own internal list. If the member says no or does not know, write mail and email touches only.
- **Texts.** A vendor list never carries texting consent. Write no text touch for anyone from a vendor list unless the member has their own documented consent for that person.
- **Dedupe against the CRM** by phone, then email, then address. Show who is already a contact, who is marked do-not-disturb, and who the member already works with, and move current clients to /hi5-re-sphere or /hi5-re-seller-updates.
- **Keep the list in the chat.** Do not save owners' names, phone numbers, or emails into Notion. Logging into the CRM is the member's choice, after the plan and OK, through /hi5-re-crm (job 3 for a new contact, with the source noted as the vendor or "own prospecting").

## Choosing and reviewing a farm
A farm is chosen on business facts. Ask only about what the CRM, the Neighborhood page, and the profile did not answer.
- **Allowed criteria:** the number of homes, turnover rate (what share sold in the last 12 months, from numbers the member provides), the price band the member serves, the member's own past sales and listings there, how far it is from the member's usual routes, the member's budget and time, and the housing type the member specializes in.
- **Never:** choose, rank, add, or drop a farm by who lives there, by a protected class, by an area's reputation, or by anything like "a family neighborhood" or "an affluent area". If the member asks for that, say which rule, and offer the business criteria above.
- **Size.** Offer a range the member can cover each month (for example by the number of mailers they can afford and doors they can visit), based on what the member tells you. Never state a standard size.
- **The farm plan** is a table: Month, Mail piece, Door-knock or event, Local note, and Response offer, for 12 months, rotating three themes (a just-sold story only where the member's MLS and brokerage rules allow it, a market update using numbers the member provides, and a local note). Include the cost per month if the member gave a budget.
- **Measure** with numbers the member provides (responses, conversations, listing appointments), and never promise a result.

## Mail and door rules
- Mail pieces carry the member's name, brokerage, the disclosure line, and the required notices. They never say "sold" for a listing the member did not sell, never use a sold price unless `sold_price_policy` and the MLS rules allow it, and never use another brokerage's listing as the member's.
- Before any door-knock session, remind the member to check local solicitation rules and any HOA rules, skip homes posted "No Soliciting", and log any "do not come back" as an internal do-not-contact request.
- Leave-behinds describe the market and the member's offer, never the people.
