# The Six Jobs

Used by `SKILL.md`. For every job: scan first (the CRM record, the profile, earlier Marketing Hub rows, the contract, builder documents, and survey the member gave), ask only about what could not be found, and point to what you found. Zoning, costs, and timelines come from documents and professionals. Drafts only. If something important is missing after the scan, ask up to 3 questions before writing.

---

## Land

### 1. The land due diligence plan
Start from the land module in `../../hi5-re-buyer/modules/land.md` and extend it for the member's role (buyer's agent or owner's agent). Inputs to look for: the parcel (address, acreage, parcel number the member gives), the buyer's or owner's purpose, the contract dates, and the documents in hand.
Build a table: the item (survey and boundaries, legal access and easements, utilities available at the lot line and what connection requires, water and septic or sewer, soils and percolation where relevant, flood and drainage, zoning and permitted uses as a question for the planning office, deed restrictions and association rules, taxes and special districts, mineral and water rights, environmental concerns, and the title commitment), the owner (the buyer, the owner, a professional, the planning office, title, or the member), the document or quote needed, the due date from the contract or "confirm with contract", and the status. Add the questions for the planning office and each professional, written so the member can send them. Never answer a zoning or buildability question, and never state costs.

### 2. Land listing and sale support
Inputs to look for: the owner's documents (survey, title, tax, utilities, restrictions) and the owner's goals. Build an owner intake list of documents and facts to gather, a listing fact sheet from documents only (acreage, frontage, access, utilities as documented, survey details, restrictions, each with its source and "buyer to verify"), a reply guide for inquiries, and a showing safety checklist for the member (the access, the terrain, notice to the owner, and telling someone the schedule). Point to /hi5-re-listing-launch for the copy and /hi5-re-listing-appt for the listing appointment. Never state what can be built or a value.

## New construction

### 3. The new construction buyer guide
Start from `../../hi5-re-buyer/modules/new-construction.md`. Inputs to look for: the builder, the community, the stage (finished, under construction, or to be built), the contract if the buyer has one, and the move-in date the buyer wants.
Build: the builder contract questions (deposits and what they cover, the selections deadlines and who decides substitutions, how completion dates and delays are handled, the warranty and what it covers, what happens to deposits if the buyer cancels, and how the builder handles the buyer's representation), read from the contract with quotes and pages and sent to the buyer's attorney for meaning, a selections tracker (the item, the deadline from the contract, the budget the buyer set, the choice, and the status), an inspection schedule at the stages the contract allows (for example before drywall and at the final), and the final walkthrough and punch list steps, pointing to /hi5-re-transaction (new construction) for the walkthrough sheet. Completion dates are marked "estimated".

### 4. A builder and community comparison
Inputs to look for: two to five builders or communities and the materials for each (the builder's own floor plans, pricing sheets, included features lists, fee schedules, and incentive offers as documents state them). Build a side by side table of only what the documents show, with the source and date of each, and a short list of questions to ask each builder for anything missing. Compare incentives using the documents and the lender's estimate, never from memory. Never rank by who lives there, never rate schools or safety, and never recommend one. Offer the vault's new construction comparison as an email to the buyer, drafted from the same facts.

### 5. The builder relationship plan
Build, for the member: how to register a buyer with the builder (the steps the builder states, and a reminder to confirm the builder's rules on representation with the broker), the questions to ask the builder's sales team, a follow-up message after each visit (under 100 words), and a tracker of builders the member works with. Never state a builder's rules or what a state requires.

## Both

### 6. The timeline and tasks
Build one timeline from the documents (contract date, deposit dates, due diligence or selections deadlines, inspections, estimated completion, final walkthrough, and closing), each date with its source or "confirm with contract" or "estimated", and the tasks for the member and the client. Show the full list as a plan and wait for the member's OK before creating anything. Create CRM tasks with /hi5-re-crm job 5 and calendar events through the calendar connection, in batches, read each back, and report any that failed. End with: "Confirm every date against the executed document. This is not legal advice."
