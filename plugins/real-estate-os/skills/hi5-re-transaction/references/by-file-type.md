# File Types

Used by `SKILL.md` for the checklist (job 2) and for the tone of every job. Pick the section that matches the file and the side the member represents. Every task has an owner (the member, the client, the lender, title or escrow, the other agent, or someone else) and a due date taken from the executed document, or the words "confirm with contract". Never invent a date.

## Purchase, buyer side
Phases: Under contract, Inspection or option period, Appraisal and financing, Title and HOA or community documents, Pre-closing (the final 72 hours), Closing day, Post-close.
Often-slipping items: earnest money delivery, the inspection scheduling, the loan documents, homeowner's insurance, and the final walkthrough.

## Purchase, seller side
Phases: Under contract, Inspection and repair response, Appraisal, Title and HOA or community documents, Pre-closing, Closing day, Post-close.
Often-slipping items: the repair response deadline, access for the appraiser, payoff information, the HOA documents, and the move-out and keys.

## Investor purchase
Add to the buyer side: due diligence on leases and the rent roll (if the property is tenant occupied), tenant notice and estoppel steps as the documents require, the property management handoff, and attorney and CPA review items. Never state returns.

## Tenant lease
Phases: Application and screening, Lease review and signing, Deposit and first month, Move-in inspection, Key handoff and utilities, Renewal and notice dates.
Screening uses written criteria applied the same way to every applicant. Do not record household details.

## Landlord lease
Phases: Applications and written criteria, Lease signing, Deposit and first month, Move-in condition report, Key handoff, Renewal and notice dates, Move-out.
The lease is a legal document and needs attorney review.

## 55+ or age-restricted community resale
Add the community's approval steps and timeline, its resale documents, and transfer and monthly fees to the title and HOA phase. Eligibility is the community's own process. Never guess or screen by age.

## New construction
Phases: Contract and deposits, Selections deadlines, Construction milestones the contract lists (for example a pre-drywall walk), Final walkthrough and punch list, Closing, Warranty. Completion dates are the builder's estimates, never promises.

## Land
Phases: Contract, Due diligence (survey, access, utilities, zoning and environmental items for qualified professionals), Title, Closing. Never guess zoning or what can be built.

## Commercial
Phases: Letter of intent, Contract or lease, Due diligence (financials, leases, environmental, survey), Financing, Title, Closing. Attorney review is a task in every phase.

## Relocation and remote clients
Add remote signing steps, a video walkthrough option, and a clear point of contact on closing day. Time zones appear on every deadline.

## Luxury
Add confidentiality steps, a longer due diligence window if the contract allows it, and private showing and access rules.
