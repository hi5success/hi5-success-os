# AI Search Visibility Check

Used by `SKILL.md` (choice F). More people now ask AI assistants for recommendations. This check shows whether the member is being mentioned and what to improve.

## Be honest about what this is
You can check using your own web search, and the member can check the other tools by hand. The results are a **sample**, not a ranking. AI answers change from one moment to the next. Say so up front.

## Step 1: Build the questions
Write 6 to 8 questions a real customer would ask, using their market and services. For example:
- "Who is the best [service provider] in [city]?"
- "Who should I hire to [task] in [city]?"
- "[Service] in [neighborhood] recommendations"
- For real estate, questions for each client type the member serves, such as buyers, sellers, renters, investors, and commercial. Keep them about the service and the place. Never ask about who lives somewhere.
- The same questions in each other language the member serves.

## Step 2: Check
1. Run each question with your web search. Record whether the member's business is mentioned, who is mentioned instead, and which sources the answers rely on (directories, review sites, local articles, the member's own site).
2. Give the member the list so they can run the same questions in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers, and send screenshots or paste what they see. Offer screenshot help. Record those results too. Do not claim you ran a tool you did not use.

## Step 3: Output
- A table: question, tool, whether the member appears, who does, and the sources.
- What the pattern suggests. For example, if competitors appear because they are on certain directories or have more recent reviews, say so.
- Improvements, in order: consistent name, address, and phone (see the directory check), reviews, a complete Google Business Profile, clear structured data on the website, pages that answer the questions customers ask, and mentions on trusted local sites. Never promise a result.
