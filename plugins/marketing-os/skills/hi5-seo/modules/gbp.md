# Google Business Profile Audit

Used by `SKILL.md` (choice A). Read the member's status from `gbp_status` and never ask for it. It tells you how deep to go: if they said it is fully optimized, do a light audit and look for gaps. If they said barely started or none, walk them through setup first.

## What to ask (only if missing, once each)
- **The profile address.** If `gbp_url` is missing: "Paste the link to your Google Business Profile, or tell me your business name and city and I'll find it." Save `gbp_url`.
- If the member serves other languages and `languages_published` is missing, ask once which languages they publish in. Save `languages_published`.

## What to read
Read the public listing through web search and the public profile page. Some details are only visible to the owner (categories in the editor, services, attributes, insights). For those, ask for a screenshot of the editing view, with screenshot help: "Open your profile, find where you edit it, and send me a screenshot of the section."

## Checklist (score each Pass, Fix, or Missing)
1. **Verified and no duplicates.** The profile is verified and there is only one for the business.
2. **Name.** Matches the real world name used on the website and signage. No added keywords or locations (Google's rules).
3. **Categories.** One specific primary category, and relevant secondary categories. Suggest the most specific options for their kind of business (see the industry file).
4. **Address or service area, phone, and website.** Match the saved name, address, and phone exactly. The website link goes to the best page.
5. **Hours.** Complete, including holidays.
6. **Description.** Up to 750 characters, written for people: who they serve, what they do, and the areas they cover. No promotions, links, phone numbers, or outcome promises, and no steering language. Follow the Compliance Guardrails page.
7. **Services and products.** Listed with plain descriptions. No prices you cannot back up.
8. **Photos.** A logo and cover photo, real photos of the member, the team, the work, and the local area. Recent, not stock. Suggest a simple monthly photo habit.
9. **Posts.** Regular updates. Suggest a cadence the member can keep.
10. **Questions and answers.** The questions customers really ask, answered by the member.
11. **Reviews.** Count, star average, how recent, and whether the owner replies. Hand off to the reviews check (choice D) for a plan.
12. **Languages.** If they serve other languages, add a short second language paragraph to the description and publish posts in that language. Written natively.
13. **Attributes and messaging.** Any attributes that apply, and the booking or message options, if they will answer them.

## Output
- A scorecard with the result for each item, in plain words.
- The top 5 actions, easiest and highest impact first, each with where to find it in the profile (general terms and screenshot help).
- A 30 day post plan: four posts in the member's voice, in each language they publish in. Posts must follow the Compliance Guardrails and never use outcome promises or steering language.
- A starter set of 5 questions and answers to add.

## Rules
- Never suggest incentives for reviews or review gating. Google's policies do not allow either.
- Never name clients or share private deal details in posts or answers.
- Save `gbp_url` once. Save the audit as a Marketing Hub row (see `SKILL.md`).
