# Reviews Check and Plan

Used by `SKILL.md` (choice D).

## What to read
The public Google reviews through web search and the public profile (count, star average, how recent, and whether the owner replies). If the member sees more, ask for a screenshot of the reviews area with screenshot help. Read `google_reviews` if saved, and update it.

## Assess
- **Count and star average**, compared with what is typical for competitors in their market. Search for two or three nearby competitors to compare. Say it is a rough comparison.
- **Recency:** how many reviews in the last 90 days.
- **Reply rate:** how many reviews have an owner reply.
- Other platforms where clients leave reviews (Zillow, Yelp, Facebook) if relevant to their business.

## Plan to get more
- A simple habit: ask every happy client at the moment they are happiest (at closing, at project completion), with a direct link to the review page.
- A short request message in the member's voice. Offer to build a follow up sequence with /hi5-email, or to use the member's review request feature in GoHighLevel if they use it.
- Never offer incentives for reviews and never ask only happy clients (review gating). Google's policies do not allow either.
- Include the member's disclosure line only where it applies to public marketing, and never name clients in public replies.

## Replies
Offer reply templates in the member's voice for positive and critical reviews. Replies never reveal confidential client or transaction details, never argue, and never contain steering language or outcome promises. Real estate replies follow the Compliance Guardrails.

## Save
Marketing Hub row "Reviews Plan" with the date (see `SKILL.md`). Update `google_reviews`.
