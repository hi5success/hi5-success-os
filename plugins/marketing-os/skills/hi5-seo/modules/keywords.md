# Keywords and Content Plan

Used by `SKILL.md` (choice E).

## Ask (only if missing, once each)
**Current rankings** (skip if an earlier row already answers it):
> "Do you know if you currently show up in Google search for the things your customers look for?
>
> A) Yes: I show up for some searches
> B) I have no idea
> C) No: I am not ranking for anything"

**Content**:
> "Are you currently publishing blog posts or helpful local content on your website?
>
> A) Yes regularly
> B) Occasionally
> C) No: nothing yet"

Do not ask about the website platform, the Google Business Profile status, or languages. They are in the profile.

## Output

### 1. Local keyword targets
15 to 20 specific phrases built from the member's services or client types, their market, and nearby areas. Use the patterns in the industry file. Include a separate set in each other language the member serves, researched and written natively, not translated word for word.

### 2. Content topics
3 to 5 topics for local search, with a mix of guides, local resources, and answers to the questions clients ask. Topics must follow the Compliance Guardrails:
- **School district and neighborhood topics are allowed.** Good examples: how to find the school district for an address, with a link to the district's official boundary tool; how enrollment works, linking to the district; neighborhood guides about parks, trails, dining, commute routes, and the homes.
- **Never** rate or describe the quality of schools, state crime or safety as fact, or describe who lives in an area or who a place suits.
- For other industries, never promise outcomes or invent proof.

### 3. Quick wins
3 to 5 actions to do this week that move the needle fastest based on what they already have.

### 4. 90 day roadmap
Month by month actions to build local authority, including content in each language they serve.
