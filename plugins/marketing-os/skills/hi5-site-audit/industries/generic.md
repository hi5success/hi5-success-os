# Hi5 Site Audit: Generic Flow (Any Industry)

Used by `SKILL.md`. Use the member's own words for who they serve and what they sell.

## Pages to expect
- A home page, an About page, and a Contact page
- A page for each main service or offer, described by the situation it solves
- A results or testimonials page, and an FAQ
- Local or area pages if the member serves specific places (see `market_reach`)
- A blog or resources section with the questions their customers ask
Report which are missing or thin. Do not call a local page missing if the member serves a fully online audience.

## Local and topic keyword patterns to look for
- [Service] in [city] and best [service] near me, for local businesses
- [Niche] [service], for the member's specialty
- The questions customers ask, as page titles and headings
Check the title, the H1, and the first paragraph for the service and, when the page is local, the place. Include the same patterns in each other language the member publishes in.

## Schema
- Use `LocalBusiness`, `ProfessionalService`, or `Organization`, whichever fits, with the business name, address or service area, phone, website, and `sameAs` links to the member's profiles. Do not invent reviews or ratings.

## Name, address, and phone
Compare with `nap_name`, `nap_address`, and `nap_phone`. If the member serves customers online only, check the name and the contact details, and skip the address.

## Compliance scan (always High)
Read the visible copy of every audited page. Flag these as findings to review, with the page, the exact words, and a rewrite. Context matters.
- **Outcome promises:** guaranteed results, specific income, savings, or timelines.
- **Invented proof:** testimonials, statistics, or credentials the member did not provide.
- **Claims about competitors** that cannot be supported.
- **Disclosure and notices:** the member's `disclosure_line_full` (if they have one) and every notice in `required_notices` that the member said they use on the web. Never say whether a notice is legally required. Follow the member's list.
- **Industry rules:** anything the member listed in `industry_rules`, such as required disclaimers for finance, health, legal, or insurance.
- Anything about tax, legal, medical, or financial topics that reads as advice rather than preparation for a professional.

## Fix-list wording
Write the rewrite suggestions in the member's voice and follow the Compliance Guardrails page.
