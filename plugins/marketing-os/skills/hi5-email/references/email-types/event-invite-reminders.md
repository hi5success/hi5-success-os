# Event Invite and Reminder Emails

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
A webinar, live class, workshop, open house, seminar, or call, from the invite through the replay.

## Default style
Event reminder ladder: each email 60 to 150 words, one link, the same P.S. every time.

## Rules for this type
- The date, time, and time zone are in the body of every email. The same P.S. repeats them with the place or link.
- Ladder: invite, confirmation right away, 24 hours before, day of, 1 hour or 10 minutes before, replay with three takeaways, replay deadline.
- Use status tags in the subject: [tomorrow], [live now], [replay], [last call].
- A "we are live" email stays under 150 words.
- Attendees skip the "starts soon" emails and get the replay thanks. Buyers exit to after purchase.

## Template
Invite: Subject is the topic in 2 to 5 words or a question about the reader's problem. Body: the problem, then "On [DEADLINE: day, date, time, time zone] I will show you:" three bullets, when, where, cost, [LINK: register].
Confirmation: "CONFIRMED! You are registered": date, time, time zone, add to calendar, three things to prepare, a question prompt.
24 hours: "[tomorrow] [topic]": one reason to show up and the link.
Day of: "We start today at [time]".
10 minutes: "[live now] [topic]": two or three bullets and the link.
Replay: "[replay] [topic]": three takeaways, the link, the replay deadline.
P.S. on every one: date, time, time zone, place or link

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral home organizing class, the 24 hour email):
> Subject: [tomorrow] the 20 minute closet reset
> Tomorrow we reset one closet in 20 minutes, live. You will leave with a plan for yours.
> Join here: [LINK: class]
> P.S. [DEADLINE: day, date, time, time zone], online.
Notes: a status tag, one reason, one link, the same P.S. as every other email in the ladder.

## Real estate angle
Open house ladder: invite, day before, morning of, then "thanks for coming" with similar homes and one question. A seminar or workshop follows the same ladder.

## Business to business or creator angle
Confirmation with a calendar link and an agenda, then the ladder. Creators: the ladder plus a replay deadline.

## Common mistakes
- No time zone.
- Different P.S. text each time.
- A long "we are live" email.
- Sending reminders to people who already attended.
