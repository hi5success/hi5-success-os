# Nurture and Value Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
Staying useful to a list that is not ready to buy: a tip, a stat, a short lesson, or a weekly note. Also the "value" email inside a launch.

## Default style
Plain friend note, 80 to 180 words. For a regular named column, a branded value column of 270 to 450 words.

## Rules for this type
- Lead with a stat, a short scene, or a question the reader is asking, in the first two lines.
- Give one useful thing and one action they can take today. No pitch in the body except one soft line.
- The P.S. can carry one related resource. A next-tier line goes there only if the member's upsell rule says so.
- Ask for a reply at least once a month.
- A branded column keeps the same name and shape every send: a hook line, a title, the problem, the fix, a short example or math, then three actions.

## Template
Subject patterns: a specific number about the topic · a contrarian claim · the reader's own data
Preview: the benefit in one line, not a repeat of the subject
Body beats:
1. A stat or short reveal that sets up the point.
2. Two to four short lines on why it matters to the reader.
3. One action they can take: [LINK: resource] with link text like "Here is how to do it".
4. Optional: a next-tier line, only if the member's upsell rule says so.
CTA: one link
P.S.: one related resource or a deadline if a real one exists
Branded column variant: hook line, title, problem (2 lines), "Here is the fix:", example or math, "Real examples:" 3 bullets, "Actions:" 3 bullets, sign-off, P.S.

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example A (a pet groomer's list):
> Subject: 3 minutes that save a coat
> Preview: the brushing mistake most owners make
> Most matting starts at the same three spots: behind the ears, under the legs, and at the base of the tail. Two minutes with a comb in those spots, twice a week, keeps it from building up.
> Here is a short clip of how I do it: [LINK: clip].
> [Name]
> P.S. Have a coat that is already matted? Reply "help" and tell me the breed.
Notes: one tip, one link, the P.S. invites a reply that the member will answer.

Example B (a branded column, one stat and three actions) would use the same name every send and end with an "Actions:" list.

## Real estate angle
One market stat or home tip for the farm area, plus a reply prompt. Describe the market and the home, never who lives there.

## Business to business or creator angle
One stat and what to do about it, with a link to a free tool or audit. Creators: one tip, the do-it-yourself link, and a soft line for the done-for-you option.

## Common mistakes
- Two lessons in one email.
- A hard pitch in a value email.
- A subject that repeats the preview.
- Long paragraphs.
