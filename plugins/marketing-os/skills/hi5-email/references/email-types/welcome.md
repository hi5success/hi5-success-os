# Welcome Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
Someone just joined a list, signed up for a free resource, created an account, or became a member. Send it within minutes.

## Default style
Plain friend note, 60 to 160 words. For a membership or an account, add a short numbered checklist.

## Rules for this type
- One job: get the reader to take one first step that takes a few minutes. Everything else is secondary.
- Thank them in one line, then give the step with its link. No sales pitch.
- Offer two or three useful resources at most. Skip anything they cannot use this week.
- End with a reply question the member will read and answer. "I read every reply" is a commitment, so use it only if the member confirmed it, otherwise write [confirm: who reads replies]. Promise nothing beyond what the member confirms.
- For a membership, a numbered "do these four things in order" list beats a paragraph.

## Template
Subject patterns: "Welcome, here is your first step" · "You are in: start here" · a one-word subject
Preview: the result of the first step in a few words
Body beats:
1. One line of thanks.
2. "Your first step (about [N] minutes): [one action]" with [LINK: first step].
3. Two or three resources, one line each.
4. A reply question: "Hit reply and tell me [one question]."
CTA: the first step link
P.S.: optional, a second resource or "what to expect from me" in one line, only if the member supplied it

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example A (a small bookkeeping service, free checklist signup):
> Subject: your checklist is here
> Preview: the first step takes 5 minutes
> Thanks for grabbing the year-end checklist. Start with step one: gather your last three bank statements in one folder. Here is the checklist again: [LINK: checklist].
> Two more things that help: the short video on categories [LINK: video] and the template sheet [LINK: sheet].
> Hit reply and tell me what part of your books worries you most. [confirm: who reads replies]
> [Name]
Notes: one first step, the point is in the first two lines, the reply question gives the member something to learn.

Example B (a membership, account just created):
> Subject: [start here] 4 steps in order
> Welcome in. Do these four things in order: 1) set up your profile [LINK: profile], 2) watch the 5 minute tour [LINK: tour], 3) join the first live call [DEADLINE: day, date, time, time zone], 4) say hello in the group [LINK: group].
> Stuck? Reply and [Name] will help.
Notes: a numbered checklist suits a membership, and the date is a placeholder until the member gives it.

## Real estate angle
After a new lead signs up: say what to expect from the agent in one line and link a saved-search or home-value page, then ask one question about their timeline.

## Business to business or creator angle
For a company or creator: a three step account setup, or one first step plus a reply question, with the support contact named.

## Common mistakes
- Sending four welcome emails at once.
- A long about-us story before the first step.
- No clear first action.
- Selling in the welcome.
