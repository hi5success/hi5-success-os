# Book a Call Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
A service pitch or an invitation to a consultation, an audit, or a strategy call.

## Default style
Plain friend note, 120 to 220 words. For B2B with a case study, a branded value column.

## Rules for this type
- One CTA: the booking link or a reply. Do not offer both as equals.
- Open with a short stat or a confession, then why it matters, then what the team does in two to four bullets, each as one line of benefit.
- Qualify the reader in one line ("this is for people who...") and say who it is not for.
- A do-it-yourself link followed by "or have us do it for you" is allowed when the member has a done-for-you option.

## Template
Subject pattern: the problem or change in 5 to 8 words
Body beats:
1. A short stat or confession.
2. Why it matters.
3. "Here is what we do:" two to four bullets, service and one line of benefit.
4. "Could your business use this? Book a time: [LINK: calendar]"
P.S.: a second reason or a free audit link

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral web design studio):
> Subject: your site loads in 7 seconds
> Most small sites load slower than visitors will wait. [SUPPLIED STAT]
> Here is what we fix first: the images, the hosting setup, and the contact form.
> This is for owners who already get visits but few calls. If you are still building your first page, start with the free guide instead: [LINK: guide].
> Want a look at yours? Book 15 minutes: [LINK: calendar]
> P.S. I can run a free speed check first. Reply "check".
Notes: one CTA, a qualifier and a who-it-is-not-for, a real stat from the member.

## Real estate angle
"Thinking about selling in spring? I will run your home's numbers": one CTA, reply or book.

## Business to business or creator angle
An insight, one case study, and a calendar link. Creators: "want help? reply call".

## Common mistakes
- Two competing CTAs.
- Features instead of benefits.
- An invented stat.
- A long bio.
