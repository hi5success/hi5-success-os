# Abandoned Checkout Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
Someone started a checkout, a booking, or a form and did not finish, and the member has a tracked, permitted way to know it.

## Default style
Lifecycle automation, a plain note of 60 to 140 words.

## Rules for this type
- Send an hour to a day after, never at night in the reader's time zone.
- Ask what happened in a friendly way, then give three likely reasons (price, fit, timing) with one honest line for each.
- One link to resume. Add "if it was something else, hit reply".
- Only use data the member has and may use. Never imply you watched them.
- No fake urgency. A real deadline is fine, a made-up one is not.

## Template
Subject pattern: "had a thought" (use the name rarely)
Body beats:
1. "Did something come up, or did you want to think it over?"
2. "Usually it is one of three things:" price (the value math, using numbers the member supplied), fit (proof for their situation: [PROOF NEEDED] if none), life (no pressure).
3. "Pick up where you left off: [LINK: resume]".
4. "If it was something else, hit reply."

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral online class):
> Subject: had a thought
> Did something come up, or did you want to think it over? Usually it is one of three things.
> Price: [PRICE] covers [WHAT IS INCLUDED]. Fit: it is built for [WHO]. Timing: no rush, the next start date is [DEADLINE: day, date, time zone].
> Pick up where you left off: [LINK: resume]
> If it was something else, hit reply.
Notes: three honest reasons, one link, a reply invitation, the date is a placeholder.

## Real estate angle
Only with CRM tracking and consent: "Saw you looked at [address] a few times. Want a showing?" One CTA.

## Business to business or creator angle
Three likely reasons plus a resume link. Creators: same, with a reply question.

## Common mistakes
- Pressure language.
- Implying surveillance.
- Sending at 2 AM.
- More than one link.
