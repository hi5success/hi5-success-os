# Review and Referral Ask

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
After a good result: a closing, a delivered project, a finished course, or an anniversary. Also a testimonial request.

## Default style
Plain friend note, 40 to 90 words.

## Rules for this type
- Ask after the first win, not before. Software and courses: after about three weeks. Real estate: 7 to 14 days after closing, with a link straight to the review page.
- One small ask, one link. Say how long it takes ("about 30 seconds").
- Never tie a gift, discount, or anything of value to a review or a referral unless the member confirms their rules allow it and it is disclosed. A thank-you after the fact is fine.
- An anniversary note can ask for a referral in one plain line.

## Template
Subject pattern: "Can I ask a small favor?"
Body beats:
1. "You have been [using X or in your home] for [N weeks or days]."
2. "Could you leave a short review? It takes about 30 seconds."
3. [LINK: review]
4. "Really appreciate it." Name.
P.S.: optional, a thank-you the member confirmed

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral landscaping company):
> Subject: can I ask a small favor?
> You have had the new garden beds for two weeks now. Could you leave a short review? It takes about 30 seconds: [LINK: review]
> Really appreciate it.
> [Name]
Notes: it comes after the work is done, one link, an honest time estimate.

## Real estate angle
A review ask 7 to 14 days after closing with a direct review link, and a home anniversary note at one year with a plain referral ask. Check brokerage rules on gifts.

## Business to business or creator angle
An NPS style question after a success moment. Creators: an ask after three weeks, and a reward only if the member confirms it is allowed.

## Common mistakes
- Asking before the first win.
- A reward tied to a positive review.
- Several links.
- A long email.
