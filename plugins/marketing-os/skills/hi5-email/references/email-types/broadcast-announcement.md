# Broadcast Announcement Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
A one-off message to a list or a community: a call, a new resource, a schedule, a policy, or news.

## Default style
Plain friend note, 60 to 180 words. Several items: a designed digest. A community call: a member notice.

## Rules for this type
- One announcement, one job, one link.
- Topic, why it matters, the time with the time zone, and one join or read link.
- For a regular community call, keep the subject pattern the same each time.
- No pitch and no fake urgency.

## Template
Subject pattern: the topic plus a tag when useful, such as [live today] or [new]
Body beats:
1. The news in one line.
2. Why it matters to the reader.
3. When: [DEADLINE: day, date, time, time zone] if there is a time.
4. [LINK: join or read]
P.S.: the repeat of the time, or a second resource

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral community):
> Subject: [Thursday] Q&A with the team
> Thursday we are answering your questions live, with an hour for anything you send ahead.
> When: [DEADLINE: Thursday, date, time, time zone]
> Join here: [LINK: join]
> P.S. Reply with your question and we will take it first.
Notes: a status tag, one time, one link.

## Real estate angle
A brokerage or team announcement, an open house reminder, or a market update with one link.

## Business to business or creator angle
A company announcement with the date and one link. Creators: a new episode, class, or drop with one link.

## Common mistakes
- Several asks in one announcement.
- No time zone.
- A pitch hidden in news.
- A long email.
