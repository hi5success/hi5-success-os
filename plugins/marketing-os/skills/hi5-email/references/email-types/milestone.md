# Milestone and Data Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
The reader reached a number or a stage, is close to the next level, or the member sends a regular data report (a client report, a home value update).

## Default style
Lifecycle automation, under 120 words. A report is a short plain note with a few figures.

## Rules for this type
- The reader's own data is the hook. Use only data the member has and may use.
- Number, what it means, the next milestone and the gap, one action. A real deadline only if one exists.
- A report shows old to new for two or three figures, one thing to fix, and one link.
- Never invent a figure. Use [DATA: name] when it is not supplied.

## Template
Subject patterns: "You reached [MILESTONE]" · "You are [GAP] away from [NEXT LEVEL]"
Body beats (120 words or fewer):
1. The number: [DATA].
2. What it means.
3. The next milestone and the gap.
4. One action or a strategy call: [LINK: name].
P.S.: a deadline only if a real one exists
Report variant: subject "[Metric] up or down for [client or home]", preview the date range, body three figures old to new, one thing to fix, one link.

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral savings app):
> Subject: you reached [MILESTONE]
> You saved [DATA: amount] this quarter. That is [MEANING].
> You are [DATA: gap] away from the next level. One thing that helps: [ACTION].
> [LINK: next step]
Notes: the data is the hook, short, one action.

## Real estate angle
A home value change, a purchase anniversary, or a rate alert, only with data the agent really has. A monthly home value or market email to past clients.

## Business to business or creator angle
Usage milestones with the gap to the next tier. Creators: course progress.

## Common mistakes
- Invented numbers.
- A long explanation.
- Several actions.
- Pressure to upgrade.
