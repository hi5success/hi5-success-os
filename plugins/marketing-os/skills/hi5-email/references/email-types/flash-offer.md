# Flash Offer Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
A short, real, limited offer: a discount, a price that is about to rise, or a bonus that ends soon. Rare. Only with a true deadline.

## Default style
Plain friend note, 120 to 220 words.

## Rules for this type
- The deadline must be real and shown with the exact time and time zone.
- Say why the offer exists (a true reason), who it is for, what is inside in three bullets, and the price math against what the reader pays now.
- One link. The subject names the offer and the deadline.
- No fake scarcity, no countdown tricks, no "last ever" unless true.

## Template
Subject pattern: [OFFER] until [DEADLINE: day, time zone]
Preview: the deal in one line plus the end date
Body beats:
1. Why the offer exists (a true reason).
2. Who it is for.
3. What is inside, three bullets.
4. Price math: [PRICE] against what they pay now (only numbers the member supplied).
5. CTA: [LINK: offer] with the exact end time and time zone.
P.S.: "Reply with what you use now and I will tell you what you would save" (only if the member confirms they will)

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral software tool):
> Subject: [PRICE] until Friday 5 pm CT
> We are opening a few spots at the early rate because [TRUE REASON]. It is for teams that [WHO].
> Inside: [ITEM 1], [ITEM 2], [ITEM 3].
> You pay [CURRENT COST] now. This is [PRICE].
> [LINK: offer], closes [DEADLINE: day, date, time, time zone].
Notes: a true reason, a clear who, the math, the exact end time.

## Real estate angle
Rare for agents. A real deadline only, such as a rate-related event or an open house this weekend, and no claims about outcomes.

## Business to business or creator angle
A percent off with clear terms and an end date, or a price that rises after a stated date.

## Common mistakes
- An end time with no time zone.
- A deadline that changes.
- Hype words.
- Promising results.
