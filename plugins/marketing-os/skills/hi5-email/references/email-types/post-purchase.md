# After a Purchase or Booking

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
Right after someone buys, books, or signs up for something paid. Access first, then onboarding, a check-in, and a review ask.

## Default style
Lifecycle automation, 80 to 200 words per email. A warm creator or agent may use a plain friend note.

## Rules for this type
- Send ONE access email within minutes: the link, the two things to do first, and a support contact. Never four separate access emails.
- Day 1 onboarding, day 3 to 7 a check-in that names the common snag, day 14 to 21 a review or testimonial ask after the first win.
- A refund or cancel stops the sequence. A reply moves it to a person.
- The support line ("Reply and [Name] will help") is a commitment. Use it only if the member confirmed who answers, otherwise write [confirm: support contact].
- The next-tier line appears only if the member's upsell rule says so. Never invent a bonus.

## Template
Subject patterns: "You are in! Here is your access to [PRODUCT]" · "Yay! You got [PRODUCT]!"
Preview: "Start with [first action], takes [N] minutes"
Body beats:
1. One line of congratulations.
2. "Log in here: [LINK: access]".
3. "Start with these two things:" two bullets.
4. Support line: [confirm: support contact]. Use "Stuck? Reply and [Name] will help." only if the member confirmed who answers.
P.S.: a bonus or community link, only if the member supplied it

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral online course):
> Subject: you are in, start here
> Preview: first lesson takes 8 minutes
> Congrats, you are in. Log in here: [LINK: access].
> Do these two first: 1) watch lesson one, 2) download the workbook [LINK: workbook].
> [confirm: support contact]
> [Name]
Notes: one email, the link in the second line, two clear first steps, a support line that waits for the member to confirm it. Examples are shortened models, so a real email lands inside the length band.

## Real estate angle
After closing: "Congrats on [address]!", a utilities and moving checklist link, a check-in note (only if the member confirms), a review ask 7 to 14 days later, and a home anniversary at one year.

## Business to business or creator angle
A receipt with the terms and the next step, then onboarding in three steps. Creators: access, two things inside, and support.

## Common mistakes
- Several access emails at once.
- Selling in the access email.
- No support contact.
- Asking for a review before the first win.
