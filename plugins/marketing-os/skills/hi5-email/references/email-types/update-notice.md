# Update and Notice Email

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything below is a default: the member's approved emails and profile win.

## When to use
A product, pricing, policy, or schedule change. Includes feature changes, plan changes, and operational notices.

## Default style
Designed digest or member notice for a company, plain friend note for a small change.

## Rules for this type
- Start the subject with an action label: [Action Required] or [No Action Needed], then what changed.
- Say what is changing, when (the date), what the reader needs to do (or nothing), and why. Link to details and give a support contact.
- Be plain and factual. No spin and no urgency that is not real.
- Never state a legal or compliance requirement as fact. Use the member's own wording.

## Template
Subject pattern: [Action Required] or [No Action Needed] + what changed
Body beats:
1. What is changing.
2. When: [DEADLINE: day, date, time zone].
3. What you need to do (or "nothing").
4. Why.
5. [LINK: details]
6. Support: [CONTACT]

## Examples (fictional, model the shape, not the words)
Example (a neutral booking tool):
> Subject: [No Action Needed] our pricing changes on [DATE]
> From [DATE] the plan moves from [OLD PRICE] to [NEW PRICE]. You do not need to do anything. Your current rate holds until [DATE].
> Why: [TRUE REASON].
> Details: [LINK: details]. Questions: [CONTACT].
Notes: the label lets readers triage at a glance, nothing is invented.

## Real estate angle
A plain, factual note, for example a change in how a fee or a process is explained, written in the agent's own words and checked against the brokerage's guidance.

## Business to business or creator angle
A labeled notice with the date and a details link. Creators: a plan change notice.

## Common mistakes
- No action label.
- Burying the date.
- Spin.
- A legal claim the member did not make.
