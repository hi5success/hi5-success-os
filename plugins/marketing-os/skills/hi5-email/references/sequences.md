# Sequence Shapes

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 2. Each shape gives the trigger, the default length and spacing, the job of each email, the exit conditions, and the segment variants. Adjust days to the member's real dates. Spacing is at most one a day until the last 2 to 3 days of a real deadline.

## Warm launch (6 emails, over about a week)
Trigger: the member opens an offer to a warm list. Goal: a sale.
1. **Open.** "You are hearing this first." Why it was built, what it is in one line, who it is for, the early bonus and its real deadline, one link. P.S. holds the deadline.
2. **Value.** One quick win they can use today, no pitch in the body except one soft line. P.S. holds the offer link and deadline.
3. **Proof or story.** One named result (with permission, no income claims) or the story behind the method. P.S. holds the deadline. Past-buyer callback for that segment.
4. **Objections.** "The 3 things holding people back": each quoted objection with a one or two line answer. The no-time objection gets the next-tier line in the body, if the member has one. State the cost of waiting plainly before the price.
5. **What is inside and the deal.** Three to five pieces grouped by the result each creates, the price, the bonus that expires, one link. P.S. holds the deadline.
6. **Last call (deadline day).** Two or three short sends only today: a morning send, an afternoon send, and one about three hours before the close with [last call] in the subject. Every P.S. holds the exact close time with the time zone.
Type files: sales and launch for emails 1, 4, 5, and 6, nurture and value for email 2, story for email 3 (or sales and launch if it is a proof email).
Exit: a buyer leaves this sequence and enters after purchase. A clicker without a purchase gets the last call only.
Segments: one core email plus a one or two line opener each for past buyers, past consult clients, and content followers.

## Lead nurture after a free resource (5 to 7 emails)
Trigger: someone downloads or requests a free resource. Spacing: day 0, then every 2 to 3 days. Order: 1 deliver and first win, 2 a short story or a value email, 3 the next problem the resource reveals, 4 how it works and the offer, 5 proof, 6 worries answered, 7 a real deadline or direct ask. A five email run folds proof and worries into the middle emails.
Type files: welcome for email 1, nurture and value or story for the middle emails, sales and launch or book a call for the offer and the direct ask.
Exit: a purchase or a booking, or a reply that starts a conversation (move to a person).
Segments: by the resource they took.

## Welcome (1 to 3 emails)
Trigger: someone joins a list or a membership. Email 1 within minutes: one thanks, one first step that takes a few minutes, two or three resources, and a reply question. For a membership add a numbered "do these four things in order" checklist. Optional email 2 on day 2 and email 3 on day 5 that set expectations and invite a reply.
Type files: welcome for every email.
Exit: the first step is done or they buy.
Segments: by how they joined.

## After a purchase (3 to 5 emails)
Trigger: a purchase or booking. Within minutes, ONE access email: the link, the two things to do first, and a support contact. Day 1 onboarding. Day 3 to 7 a check-in that names the common snag. Day 14 to 21 a review or testimonial ask after the first win. Then a next step only if the member's upsell rule says so.
Type files: post-purchase for every email, review and referral ask for the review email.
Exit: a refund or a cancel stops the sequence. A reply moves it to a person.
Segments: by product, and DIY versus done-for-you.

## Event reminder ladder (5 to 6 emails)
Trigger: a registration. Confirm right away (date, time, time zone, add to calendar, what to prepare, a question prompt), 24 hours before, day of, 1 hour or 10 minutes before, a replay email with three takeaways, and a replay deadline. The same P.S. with date, time, time zone, and place goes in every email.
Type files: event invite and reminders for every email.
Exit: an attendee skips the "starts soon" emails and gets the replay thanks. A buyer exits to after purchase.
Segments: registered, attended, and no-show.

## Win-back (3 emails over 3 days, or a single note)
Trigger: no activity for the member's own inactivity window. With a code: day 1 thanks for the past purchase, the offer, the code, and the deadline. Day 2 the math (the normal price against theirs and what is extra). Day 3 the last call. If anything broke, say so honestly in an apology email. For free trial users or inactive subscribers send one plain note with a one-question reply instead.
Type files: win-back for every email.
Exit: any purchase or reply.
Segments: past buyers, past trial users, long inactive.

## Lifecycle triggers (one or two emails each)
- **Abandoned checkout:** a "had a thought" note an hour to a day later, with three likely reasons and a resume link.
- **Failed payment:** calm, one fix link, no blame, a second note a few days later.
- **Milestone:** the reader's number, what it means, the gap to the next one, one action.
- **Type files:** abandoned checkout, milestone, and update and notice.
- **Renewal, pricing, or policy notice:** an action label, what changes, when, what to do (or nothing), why, a details link, a support contact.
Exit: the issue is fixed.
Segments: by plan or product.
