# Selling Moves

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3 whenever the goal is a sale or a booking. These moves work alongside the type file and the rules.

## The moves
- **Lead with the change they get,** not the features. Say what their week or result looks like after.
- **Name the old way that failed.** Name a method or a habit, never a person or a competitor.
- **Offer stack by result.** Group what is included by the result each piece creates, three to five pieces, not a long list.
- **Bonuses only with real end dates.** Each bonus gets its own true date. If a date is unknown, use [DEADLINE: day, date, time, time zone].
- **Cost of waiting before the price.** State, factually, what continuing the old way costs in time or money, using numbers the member supplied. Then the price.
- **Objections in threes.** "The 3 things holding people back": each objection quoted in the reader's words, then a short honest answer.
- **One named proof story.** One person, with permission, with one number the member can back up. If there is none, write [PROOF NEEDED].
- **"What is the catch?"** When an offer looks too good, give a few true reasons it exists (why the price, why the bonus, why now). Never an invented reason.
- **Disqualify.** Say who it is not for. It builds trust and filters the wrong buyers.
- **Time versus money framing.** A buyer with more time than money gets the deal and bonus framing (do-it-yourself offers). A buyer with more money than time gets the time-saved framing (done-for-you offers).
- **Shrink the dream to a first milestone** the reader can reach this week.

## The next-tier exit ramp
If the member has a higher-touch or done-for-you offer (read from `offers`, and apply the member's upsell rule if the profile has one), add one line for it in the P.S. of sales emails, and in the body of the objection email that handles "I do not have time". If the member's profile has an upsell rule, that rule decides where the line appears, and it still never replaces the email's primary call to action. The P.S. may hold the deadline and the next-tier link together, since the next-tier line is not a second call to action. The line never replaces the email's primary call to action, and it never appears in value, welcome, or post-purchase emails unless the member's upsell rule says so. No higher tier, no line.

## Guardrails
- Refund and guarantee wording must match the member's terms exactly, word for word. If it is not supplied, write [GUARANTEE: paste terms].
- No "last chance" or "last ever" unless it is true. A deadline that comes back is not a last chance.
- No income claims, and none at all for real estate or agent audiences.
- Proof is the member's own, with permission, and public. Internal proof stays out.
- Never invent a price, bonus, link, deadline, testimonial, or result.
