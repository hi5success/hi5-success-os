# Quality Check

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 4 (before anything is shown) and in audit mode. Check each item, fix every failure, and only then deliver. In audit mode, run the same list on the member's pasted email, then score it.

## Checklist
1. One job and one primary call to action.
2. The point is in the first 2 lines.
3. The preview text differs from the subject and adds something.
4. Length is inside the band for the style and type.
5. The style matches the plan the member approved.
6. Every price, deadline, bonus, link, guarantee, and proof item came from the profile or this conversation. Anything else is a placeholder, and the placeholders are listed.
7. No invented commitments: no "I will send", "we will call", or date the member did not give.
8. The footer is complete: the member's full disclosure line, any required notices, an unsubscribe line ([LINK: unsubscribe] if no platform merge tag is known), and the member's physical mailing address (from the Compliance page or `nap_address`, or the address already inside the disclosure line). If the address is missing or hidden, [MAILING ADDRESS] is in place and flagged.
9. No em dashes anywhere.
10. Nothing from the AI-tell list in the rules file.
11. The generic-copy detector passes: read `../hi5-funnel/references/quality-gates.md` and use only its generic-copy detector section to rewrite any line a competitor could say word for word. Ignore its other file references.
12. Frequency and time zone follow the rules (at most one a day until the last 2 to 3 days of a real deadline, the audience's time zone).
13. Compliance categories for the offer (`offer_categories`) are met. For a real estate member, the Fair Housing scan in the real estate industry file passes.
14. The next-tier line appears only where the member's upsell rule allows it, and in sales emails never as the primary call to action.

15. Formatting matches the style the member chose for this campaign (A plain note, B light, C designed).
16. The P.S. is italic in styles B and C.
17. Every results claim or statistic (including the member's own sales count) has an asterisk and its small-print disclaimer at the bottom.
18. No dash lists, and no underline on anything that is not a link (unless the member asked for it in this email).
19. Bold and emoji counts are inside the limits and the chosen option (bold at most 4, emoji as chosen).
20. The HTML body renders with proper line breaks (separate p and li lines, not one long line), uses inline styles only, and has no scripts or external CSS. A plain-text version is included.

## Score and fix
Give the draft a score from 1 to 10 and name the three most important improvements. Fix them before delivering. In audit mode, show the score, the three fixes, and before and after rewrites of the subject, the preview text, and the opening lines. Never add a claim the member did not supply. A price, date, or number in a pasted email stays only if the member confirms it, otherwise it becomes a placeholder in the rewrite.
