# Hi5 Email: Real Estate Flow

Used by `SKILL.md`.

## OPENING

Read from the Master Profile: `name`, `niche`, `primary_market`, `client_categories`, `behavioral_style`, `crm`.

> "Let's build an email sequence that sounds like you and converts. I already know your market is [primary_market] and you work with [client_categories]. A few quick questions and I will write the whole thing."

## SEQUENCE SELECTION

> "What type of email sequence do you need?
>
> A) New lead nurture: someone just entered my database
> B) Buyer nurture: leads not ready to buy yet
> C) Seller nurture: homeowners thinking about selling
> D) Past client / sphere: staying top of mind
> E) Post-close follow up: after the transaction closes
> F) Open house follow up: leads from an open house
> G) Something else: I will describe it
> H) Renter or landlord nurture
> I) Investor nurture
> J) Commercial client nurture"

Only offer the choices that fit the client types in `client_categories`, plus A, D, E, and G.

## SEQUENCE DETAILS

**Q1: Length**
> "How many emails in this sequence?
>
> A) 3 emails: quick follow up series
> B) 5 emails: standard nurture
> C) 7 emails: full campaign
> D) 10+ emails: long term nurture"

**Q2: Timing**
> "How should the emails be spaced?
>
> A) Daily for the first week
> B) Every 2-3 days
> C) Weekly
> D) Mix: frequent at first then taper off"

**Q3: Goal**
> "What is the goal of this sequence?
>
> A) Book a call or consultation
> B) Get them to a showing or open house
> C) Stay top of mind until they are ready
> D) Get a referral or review"

## CONTENT BY SEQUENCE TYPE

- New lead: market insight, a value offer, and a consultation CTA
- Buyer nurture: market education, a listings preview, and readiness tips
- Seller nurture: market data, a home value offer, and success stories
- Past client or sphere: a personal touch, a market update, and a referral ask
- Post close: a check in, a review request, and a referral program
- Open house: a follow up, similar listings, and a consultation offer
- Renter or landlord: how the rental process works, the full move-in or ownership costs, and a clear next step
- Investor: how to evaluate a deal, market and rent data, and a request for their buy criteria. Never promise returns
- Commercial: market and space or property updates, what to prepare, and a request for their requirements. Never guess at lease or legal terms

Describe the market, the property, and the amenities. Never describe who lives somewhere.
