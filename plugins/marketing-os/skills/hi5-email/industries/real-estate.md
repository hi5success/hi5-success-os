# Hi5 Email: Real Estate Flow

Used by `SKILL.md`.

## OPENING

Read from the Master Profile: `name`, `niche`, `primary_market`, `client_categories`, `offers`, `agent_facing_offers`, `crm`.

If `agent_facing_offers` is yes (the member markets to other agents), skip the client menu below and use the business to business notes in the generic industry flow: write for agents as business owners (time saved, the revenue process, proof from similar businesses), with no income claims.

> "Let's write the email that gets the job done. I already know your market is [primary_market] and you work with [client_categories]. Tell me who it is for and what you want it to do, and I will fill in the rest."

## SEQUENCE SELECTION

> "Who is the email or sequence for?
>
> A) New lead nurture: someone just entered my database
> B) Buyer nurture: leads not ready to buy yet
> C) Seller nurture: homeowners thinking about selling
> D) Past client / sphere: staying top of mind
> E) Post-close follow up: after the transaction closes
> F) Open house follow up: leads from an open house
> G) Something else: I will describe it
> H) Renter or landlord nurture
> I) Investor nurture
> J) Commercial client nurture
> K) Free resource follow-up (five to seven emails after someone downloads or requests something)
> L) After a consultation or booking
> M) Event or workshop follow-up
> N) Audit an email I already have (paste it)"

Only offer the choices that fit the client types in `client_categories`, plus A, D, E, G, and N. When the trigger is a download or a request for a free resource, use K. Otherwise use the choice for the client type (for example C for sellers). Then ask the goal: book a consultation, get to a showing or an open house, stay top of mind, or get a referral or review.

## REAL ESTATE ANGLES BY TYPE

- **Nurture and value:** one home tip or one local market stat for the farm area, plus a reply prompt. Lead with a question the reader is asking, or with [SUPPLIED STAT: source and date] only if the member provided it. A named client result needs the client's permission and a number the member can back up.
- **Book a call or consultation:** "Thinking about selling in the spring? I will run your home's numbers." One call to action: reply, book, or view the listing.
- **Welcome after a new lead:** what to expect from the agent in one line and a saved-search or home-value link.
- **Post-close:** a thank-you, a utilities and moving checklist, a review ask 7 to 14 days after closing with a direct review link, and a home anniversary note with a plain referral ask at one year. Check the brokerage's rules on gifts, and never tie anything of value to a review or referral.
- **Open house ladder:** invite, the day before, the morning of, then a "thanks for coming" follow-up with similar homes and one question.
- **Monthly past-client note:** a short market or home value note with two or three figures, only with data the agent really has.
- **Abandoned or milestone:** "Saw you looked at [address] a few times. Want a showing?" only with CRM tracking and consent.
- **Win-back:** "Are you still thinking about moving?" one question, reply yes or no.
- **Story:** a client move story (with permission) or why the agent got into real estate.

Describe the market, the property, and the amenities. Never describe who lives somewhere.

## FAIR HOUSING SCAN

Run every draft through this scan before it is shown, and fix any line that fails. Say in one line what you changed.
- No steering: nothing that suggests an area, a school, or a building is better or worse for a kind of person.
- No preference or limitation: nothing that favors or discourages any group, and no wording that points to a protected class (race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, and any others in the member's state, see the Compliance page).
- Never describe who lives in an area, and never rate schools or safety. Point to official sources instead.
- Describe the home, the amenities, and the place, not the people they suit.
- Segments and send lists use business facts (what someone downloaded, when they last replied), never who someone is or where they are from.
