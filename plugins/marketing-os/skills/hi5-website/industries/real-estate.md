# Hi5 Website: Real Estate Flow

Used by `SKILL.md`.

## OPENING

Read from the Master Profile: `name`, `business_name`, `primary_market`, `niche`, `client_categories`, `role`, `years_in_business`, `behavioral_style`, `success_vision`.

> "Let's write website copy that sounds like you and converts visitors into leads. I already know your market, niche, and brand voice. Which page are we writing?
>
> A) Home page: the main landing page
> B) About page: your story and credibility
> C) A page for one client type (I'll ask which)
> D) Contact page: drive them to reach out
> E) Full website: write all pages"

For client type pages, offer a page for each type in `client_categories`: Buyer, Seller, Renter, Landlord or property management, Investor, Luxury, 55+ communities, Relocation, New construction, Commercial, Land.

## PAGE OUTPUTS

### Home Page
- Hero headline and subheadline (3 options)
- Value proposition paragraph
- 3 "why work with me" reasons
- Social proof placeholder (reviews and stats)
- Primary CTA section
- Secondary CTA section

### About Page
- Personal story opening (warm, human, specific to their market)
- Credibility section (years in business, transactions, market expertise)
- Why I do this (connects to their success vision from /hi5-self)
- Community connection (local market knowledge)
- Personal details (family, hobbies: makes them real)
- CTA to connect

### Client Type Pages (one per type)
- A headline that speaks to that client's goals and worries in the member's market
- What working with me looks like (process overview)
- Why clients of this type choose me
- An FAQ with 3 to 5 questions specific to the market and the client type
- CTA to book a consultation

Examples of what to include by type: for buyers, a first-time buyer section if relevant. For sellers, the listing approach and results placeholder. For renters, how the search and application work and the full move-in costs. For landlords, what leasing and management cover. For investors, how a deal is evaluated, without promising returns. For commercial and land, the process and what to prepare, and never guess at legal or zoning terms.

### Contact Page
- A warm opening that reduces friction
- What happens after they reach out
- Response time promise
- Multiple contact options
- CTA button copy (3 options)

Local market references woven into every page. Describe places, homes, and amenities. Never describe residents, school quality, or safety as fact.
