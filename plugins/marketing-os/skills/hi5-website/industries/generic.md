# Hi5 Website: Generic Flow (Any Industry)

Used by `SKILL.md`. Use the member's own words for who they serve and what they sell.

## OPENING

Read from the Master Profile: `name`, `business_name`, `industry`, `niche`, `offer`, `primary_market`, `role`, `years_in_business`, `behavioral_style`, `success_vision`.

> "Let's write website copy that sounds like you and converts visitors into leads. I already know your offer, niche, and brand voice. Which page are we writing?
>
> A) Home page: the main landing page
> B) About page: your story and credibility
> C) Services page: what you offer (one page or one per service)
> D) Results page: proof, reviews, and case studies
> E) Contact page: drive them to reach out
> F) FAQ page
> G) Full website: write all pages"

## PAGE OUTPUTS

### Home Page
- Hero headline and subheadline (3 options)
- Value proposition paragraph
- 3 "why work with me" reasons
- Social proof placeholder (reviews and stats)
- Primary CTA section
- Secondary CTA section

### About Page
- Personal story opening (warm, human, specific)
- Credibility section (years in business, results, expertise)
- Why I do this (connects to their success vision from /hi5-self)
- Personal details that make them real
- CTA to connect

### Services Page
- A headline about the outcome the client wants
- What is included and how the process works
- Who it is for, described by situation
- A short FAQ
- CTA to book a call or start

### Results Page
- Proof placeholders: reviews, stats, and short case studies. Use only results the member gives you

### Contact Page
- A warm opening that reduces friction
- What happens after they reach out
- Response time promise
- Multiple contact options
- CTA button copy (3 options)

### FAQ Page
- 5 to 8 questions their customers actually ask, with clear answers. Send anything about tax, legal, medical, or financial matters to the right professional.
