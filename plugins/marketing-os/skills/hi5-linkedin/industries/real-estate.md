# Hi5 LinkedIn: Real Estate Flow

Used by `SKILL.md`.

> "LinkedIn is one of the most underused platforms for real estate agents, especially for reaching referral partners, investors, business owners, and people relocating for work. Let's create content that builds your professional brand there. What are we working with?
>
> A) A YouTube video or script
> B) A blog post
> C) A client win or success story
> D) A market insight or opinion
> E) A personal story from my business journey
> F) Something else"

Target the audience that fits the member's client types in `client_categories`: referral partners, investors, relocating professionals, business owners and commercial tenants or buyers. Use professional insight, not property listings. Never describe residents of an area.
