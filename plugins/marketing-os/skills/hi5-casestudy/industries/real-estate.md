# Hi5 Case Study: Real Estate Flow

Used by `SKILL.md`.

> "The best thing you can show a potential client is proof that you have done it before, for someone in a similar situation. Let's turn one of your wins into a story that sells. Tell me about the deal or client situation you want to feature."

**Q1: Client type**
> "What type of client was this?
>
> A) First time buyer
> B) Move up buyer
> C) Seller
> D) Investor
> E) Relocation client
> F) Luxury buyer or seller
> G) Renter or tenant
> H) Landlord
> I) Commercial client
> J) Active adult (55+) community buyer or seller
> K) Land or new construction client"

Offer the choices that fit `client_categories`. Describe the situation and the property, not the person. For a 55+ community, talk about the community and the move, never the client's age.
