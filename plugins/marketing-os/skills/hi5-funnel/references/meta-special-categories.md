# Meta Special Ad Categories

Used by `SKILL.md` for every ad step. Meta requires advertisers to declare certain kinds of ads as a Special Ad Category and limits how those ads can be targeted. The rules change, so every ad step ends with: "Check Meta's current policy before you launch." Drafts only. Never connect to an ad account or publish.

## Which category applies
Ask, or read `offer_categories`, and match the offer:

| If the ad promotes | Category | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Homes for sale, rentals, real estate services, or anything closely related | Housing | Rentals are Housing too. Commercial real estate is unclear, so the cautious default is to treat it as Housing unless the member checks Meta's current policy and confirms it does not apply |
| A job, a career, or a place on a team, including recruiting agents | Employment | |
| Credit, loans, mortgages, or similar financial products | Credit and financial products | |
| Social issues, elections, or politics | Social issues, elections, or politics | Needs authorization and a "paid for by" disclaimer instead of the targeting limits below |
| None of these | No Special Ad Category | Still follow Meta's general ad policies and any limits for health and wellness |

When an offer fits more than one (for example a mortgage offer from a real estate agent), declare every category that fits.

## The limits that come with Housing, Employment, and Credit
Draft the audience and setup instructions to follow these:
- No targeting by age.
- No targeting by gender.
- No targeting by ZIP or postal code.
- A location radius of at least about 15 miles around the area served.
- Do not use targeting options or lookalike audiences built on those traits. Meta may offer broader replacement audiences. Use what Meta allows for the category.
- Let the creative and the offer do the work of reaching the right people, not narrow targeting.

## Creative and copy rules for category ads
- Describe the offer, the property, the place, and the amenities. Never describe or imply who an ad is for by protected class or by who lives somewhere.
- No steering words ("perfect for families", "ideal for young professionals").
- No school ratings, crime, or safety claims as fact.
- No promised outcomes, guaranteed prices, or quoted rates.
- Images show homes, places, and the member. Do not stage images to suggest a type of resident.
- The landing page must match the ad and carry the disclosure line.

## Special case: communities with an age rule
A 55+ or active adult community may state its own age rule as the community's rule, if the member supplies it. The ad copy talks about the community, its amenities, and its rules. Because age targeting is not allowed, the creative and the offer carry the message. Never assume anything about a viewer's age, health, or family.

## Launch checklist (include in every ad pack where a category applies)
1. The campaign declares the right Special Ad Category.
2. The location is a radius of at least about 15 miles, or a broader area. No ZIP codes.
3. No age or gender targeting, and no targeting built on those traits.
4. The ad text and images pass the copy rules above.
5. The ad carries the short disclosure line, or links to a page with the full line.
6. The landing page headline matches the ad promise.
7. The member has checked Meta's current policy for their category.
For ads outside a Special Ad Category, keep items 4, 5, 6, and 7 and add: the member has checked Meta's rules for their industry.

## Never
Never write an ad that targets or excludes people by a protected trait, and never offer instructions for getting around a category's limits.
