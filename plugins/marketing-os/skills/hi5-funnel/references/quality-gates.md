# Quality Gates and Audit Scoring

Used by `SKILL.md` before any asset is saved, and for scoring in Audit mode. Fix every failure before presenting the asset.

## Generic-copy detector
Find and rewrite any line that:
- Any competitor in any industry could say word for word.
- Leans on empty buzzwords such as "supercharge", "revolutionize", "best in class", "turnkey", "one-stop shop", "holistic", "leverage", "innovative", "state of the art", "elevate", or "passionate about".
- Explains how it works when it should describe what the buyer gets.
- Contains no number, time frame, named thing, or concrete picture.

## Copy checklist
1. It passes the quick clarity test (`frameworks.md`).
2. The small result appears in the headline or the first line.
3. A specific old method or frustration is named, never a person or competitor.
4. The buyer's own words are used, taken from real phrases.
5. Length matches how ready the buyer is.
6. The first screen works for someone ready to act.
7. Every section answers a real question or concern.
8. The offer path and guarantee match what the member chose, and the honesty rule is met.
9. Urgency and scarcity are true.
10. Every claim can be shown. Anything unsupported is marked `[PROOF NEEDED]`.
11. The voice matches the Voice Profile. No em dashes unless the member allows them.
12. Nothing invented: no testimonial, number, result, or credential the member did not give.

## Compliance gates (load from `compliance-by-industry.md`)
Run the gates for each rule category that applies to the member's offer.
- **Everyone:** testimonials are real and permitted. A connection that could sway a reader is disclosed near the claim. Typical results are not overstated. No internal proof in a public asset.
- **Disclosure:** every page and email ends with `disclosure_line_full` and any `required_notices` the member uses on the web. Ads carry `disclosure_line_short` or link to a page with the full line.
- **Health and wellness:** no health or medical outcome claims, no speaking to a person's condition, no before and after result claims.
- **Income:** no earnings claims or implied ones unless supplied by the member, marked public, and shown with a disclaimer.
- **Special Ad Category:** the right category is declared, and the targeting limits are written into the ad setup (`meta-special-categories.md`).
- **Real estate (only for real estate members):** the Fair Housing scan and rules in `industries/real-estate.md`.
- **Texting:** a phone field only with documented consent and a consent checkbox.

## Audit mode scoring
1. Run every item in the copy checklist and the compliance gates. Mark each pass or fail.
2. Give an overall score from 1 to 10 and the three most important fixes.
3. Quote the weakest lines, say why each fails, and show the rewrite as before and after. At minimum rewrite the headline, subhead, first screen, and button.
4. For ads and emails, rewrite the hook or subject line and the first lines of the body.
5. Never add a claim the member did not supply.
