# Hi5 Funnel: Any Industry Adapter

Used by `SKILL.md` for every member who is not in real estate, and as the starting point for anything else. This is a full flow. It builds the funnel menu from the member's own offers and buyers, and asks for anything the profile does not have. Nothing here is a fixed list of lead magnets. Every free resource comes from what the member actually offers.

## Step 1: Build the funnel menu from the profile
Read these from the Master Profile: `offers` (one line per offer: name, who buys it, typical price), `offer`, `niche`, `industry`, `client_situations`, `differentiator`, `proof_point` (and `proof_point_public`), `client_words`, `primary_market`, `market_reach`, `website_platform`, and `crm`.

If `offers` is missing, ask once and save it:
> "What are the one to three main things you sell? For each one, tell me who buys it and about what it costs."
(If `offer` already describes one thing, confirm it and ask only for the buyer and price.)

Then offer the menu, built from their own offers:
> "Which funnel should we build first?
>
> A) [Offer 1] for [who buys it]
> B) [Offer 2] for [who buys it]
> C) [Offer 3] for [who buys it]
> D) Something else: I'll describe it"
Recommend the offer that is easiest to sell first or has the clearest buyer, and say why. Build one funnel at a time.

## Step 2: Pick the funnel format from the offer
Choose the format that fits the offer and its price, and say why:
| Offer | Funnel format |
|---|---|
| A service people hire after a conversation or estimate | Free resource, or a quote or booking request, leading to a call |
| A high-touch program, coaching, or consulting | Free resource or short assessment, then an application or a call |
| A lower-priced product or course | A free sample leading to the product page, with checkout |
| Software or an app | A free trial or a starter template with a first-win path |
| A physical or local product | A buying guide or quiz leading to the product or a visit |
| An event, workshop, or webinar | A registration page, reminders, and a follow-up |
Use the offer path that matches (see `references/offer-paths.md`).

## Step 3: Build the free resource from the offer
Never use a stock idea. Ask:
> "What is the first small win a buyer could get from you in about fifteen minutes or less, using something you already know or do?"
Offer three options drawn from their answer and their offer, in one of these forms: a checklist, a scorecard or short assessment, a calculator, a template, a short training, a sample of the real work, or a trial. The resource must give a real quick win and then point honestly to the next problem the offer solves.
Illustrations of the pattern only (always derive from the member's offer): a heating and cooling company might give a seasonal home checklist, a medical spa might give a guide to preparing for a consultation that makes no results claims, a coach might give a self-assessment scorecard, a software company might give a starter template with a short first-win path.

## Step 4: The adapter (build it once, save it, reuse it)
Check the Marketing Hub for a row titled "Funnel Adapter" for this member. If one exists, use it and ask "Still right?". If not, build it with the member, one question at a time, using the six parts in `references/frameworks.md` (section 11):
1. **Buyers and what each wants.** Use `offers` and `client_situations`.
2. **Outdated advice.** "What have your buyers already tried or been told that did not work?" Aim for up to ten.
3. **Small visible wins.** Up to fifteen results a buyer could see, drawn from the offer and the member's proof. Never invent one.
4. **Words and jargon.** Use `client_words` and the Voice Profile. List jargon to avoid.
5. **Trust signals.** Credentials, licenses, years, results marked public, affiliations, and reviews.
6. **Compliance categories.** Read or ask for `offer_categories` (see `references/compliance-by-industry.md`).
Save the result as a Marketing Hub row (Type Funnel, Title "Funnel Adapter" and the industry).

## Step 5: Industry notes to check against `compliance-by-industry.md`
- **Licensed trades and local services** (for example heating and cooling, plumbing, electrical): include the license number the member gives in the disclosure. Never promise savings or timelines you cannot support.
- **Health and wellness, medical spas, clinics:** no health or medical outcome claims.
- **Coaching, consulting, and business programs:** no income or earnings claims unless the member supplies them and marks them public, with a disclaimer.
- **Software and digital products:** state refund and cancellation terms plainly, link the privacy policy, and use only real scarcity.
- **Professional services (legal, tax, financial, insurance):** the member's licensing and industry rules apply, and nothing reads as advice.
- **Online stores:** claims must be supportable, and shipping and return terms are stated.

## Step 6: The conversion action
Pick the single action the funnel drives: book a call, request a quote, start a trial, buy, or apply. One funnel, one action.

## Step 7: Platform
Read `website_platform` and `crm`. If they are missing, ask once which page builder and email tool the member uses, and save `website_platform`. This tailors the "build it" steps.
