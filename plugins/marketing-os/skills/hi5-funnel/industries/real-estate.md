# Hi5 Funnel: Real Estate Adapter

Used by `SKILL.md` ONLY when `industry_flow` is `real-estate`. Real estate members serve many kinds of clients, so the funnel menu comes from `client_categories`, not from listings. Everything in `SKILL.md` still applies. This file adds real estate rules and the funnel menu.

## Step 1: Build the funnel menu from the member's client types
Read `client_categories`, `niche`, `primary_market`, `surrounding_areas`, `price_range`, `offers`, `proof_point` (and `proof_point_public`), and the Objection Bank. Offer a funnel for each client type the member serves:
> "Which kind of client should we build a funnel for first?" (list only the types in `client_categories`)

For each type, the free resource comes from what THIS member already gives or does for that client, so ask:
> "What do you already do or give [client type] at the start that helps them? What small win could they get from you in about fifteen minutes?"
Then use the pattern below as a starting point, never as fixed copy.

| Client type | Starting pattern for the free resource | Funnel action | Ad category notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyers and first-time buyers | A readiness check or roadmap built around the member's process | Buyer consultation | Housing |
| Sellers | A request for a home value conversation based on recent local sales | Listing consultation | Housing |
| Renters | A budget and move-in cost checklist | A search brief or a showing | Housing (rental ads) |
| Landlords | A rent pricing review for their property | Leasing or management call | Housing (rental ads) |
| Investors | A worksheet for checking a deal, or an intake of their buying criteria | Investor call | Housing. Never promise returns |
| 55+ communities | A comparison guide to communities: amenities, HOA fees, and each community's own rules | Community tour | Housing. Age targeting is not allowed, so the creative and the offer carry the message |
| Luxury | A private market report or a discreet valuation conversation | Private consultation | Housing |
| Commercial (sales and leases) | A checklist of space needs and total occupancy cost | A requirements call | Cautious default: treat as Housing unless the member checks Meta's current policy and confirms otherwise |
| Relocation | A guide to the area using commute, costs, and official sources | A planning call | Housing |
| New construction | A list of questions to ask a builder, and the tip to contact the agent before the first visit | A builder visit plan | Housing |
| Land | A due diligence checklist for a parcel | A property review call | Housing |

Funnel formats real estate members often want: a **quiz funnel** (a short readiness quiz about timing and situation, never about who the person is), and a **home value page funnel** for sellers.

## Step 2: Agent-facing funnels (only if the member says so)
Never ask whether the member recruits agents or sells to agents, and never assume it. Offer an agent-facing funnel ONLY if the member has said so: in this conversation, or in the profile (the Content Profile channel goal mentions attracting agents, `offer` or `offers` describe products, coaching, or tools for agents, or `agent_facing_offers` is yes). Then:
- Treat it as the any-industry adapter (see `industries/generic.md`) with the real estate rules below.
- No earnings figures or income examples unless the member supplies them, marks them public, and a disclaimer line is shown (see `references/compliance-by-industry.md`).
- Do not name a brokerage unless the member did.
- Ads that offer a career or a place on a team fall under the Employment Special Ad Category.
- Agent-facing funnels are separate from client funnels. Never mix the two audiences in one asset.

## Step 3: Real estate compliance (always applies)
Read the Compliance Guardrails page, including `disclosure_line_full`, `disclosure_line_short`, `required_notices`, `protected_class_jurisdictions`, `brokerage_ad_rules`, and `sms_consent_status`.
- **Fair Housing language.** Describe the property, the place, and the amenities. Never describe or imply who a home or area is for, or who lives there. No steering words ("perfect for families", "ideal for young professionals", "safe neighborhood", "great church nearby"). No school ratings, crime data, or flood zones as fact. School district content is fine when it points to official sources and never rates schools.
- **Housing Special Ad Category.** Every ad for homes, rentals, and related real estate offers declares it, and follows the targeting limits in `references/meta-special-categories.md`. Rental ads are included. Commercial keeps the cautious default.
- **No promised outcomes.** No guaranteed sale, price, appraisal, or timeline, and no quoted rates. A guarantee in a real estate funnel is a service commitment only (a reply time, an update schedule, a clear process).
- **Disclosure.** Every page and email ends with `disclosure_line_full` plus the notices in `required_notices` that the member uses on the web. Ads use `disclosure_line_short`. Never say whether a notice is legally required.
- **Data and photos.** Use only listing data and photos the member is licensed to use and follow their MLS rules.
- **Brokerage rules.** Follow `brokerage_ad_rules`, such as team name use and ad approval.
- **Texting.** A phone field only if `sms_consent_status` is yes, with a consent checkbox. Otherwise email only.
- **Tax, legal, and lending questions** go to the member's CPA, attorney, or lender.
- **Proof.** Closed-deal numbers and testimonials need the client's permission. Never use a proof point marked internal in a public asset.
- Run the Fair Housing scan on every asset before saving (steering words, school or safety claims stated as fact, outcome promises, quoted rates).

## Vocabulary and trust signals
Words clients use: pre-approval, closing costs, offer, inspection, listing, showing, move-in costs, deposit, lease, cap rate, build-out, HOA. Avoid jargon the client has not used. Trust signals: license details as licensed, years in the market, deals closed (with permission), reviews, local knowledge shown through specifics, and the brokerage name as licensed.

## Outdated advice to listen for (adapt to the member's market)
Waiting for a perfect moment. Chasing the lowest fee instead of the full plan. Relying only on online listings. Skipping a clear plan before showings or a listing. For the member's own clients, use the Objection Bank and the real phrases they hear, never a stock list.
