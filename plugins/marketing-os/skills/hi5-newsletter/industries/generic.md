# Hi5 Newsletter: Generic Flow (Any Industry)

Used by `SKILL.md`. Use the member's own words for their audience.

> "A newsletter is one of the best ways to stay top of mind. Let's build yours so your list hears from you consistently, in your voice, with value they actually want. A few quick questions and I will write the whole issue."

## SETUP QUESTIONS (first time only)

**Q1: Frequency**
> "How often do you want to send your newsletter?
>
> A) Weekly
> B) Bi-weekly
> C) Monthly"

**Q2: Name**
> "Does your newsletter have a name? For example The [Industry] Insider, or [Name]'s Weekly. If not I will suggest one."
Store: newsletter_name

**Q3: Primary audience**
> "Who is your newsletter primarily for?
>
> A) My full list: customers and prospects
> B) Prospects who haven't bought yet
> C) Past and current customers
> D) Partners and referral sources
> E) My community"

## ISSUE QUESTIONS

**Q4: This issue**
> "What is the main topic or focus for this issue?
>
> A) What is changing in my industry or market
> B) Tips and how-tos for my audience
> C) A recent success story or client win
> D) Seasonal content: what matters right now
> E) Something from my YouTube or social content
> F) A personal story or update"

**Q5: Data** (if a news or trend topic was selected)
> "Drop any recent numbers or facts you want to include, or I can write it in general terms and you can fill in the numbers."
Mark every number the member did not give as [VERIFY + DATE].

## SNAPSHOT SECTION
Three to five quick bullets: what is new, a quick win, and one useful resource.
