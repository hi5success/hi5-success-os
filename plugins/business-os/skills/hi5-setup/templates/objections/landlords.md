# Starter Objections: Landlords

Used by Stage 4 of /hi5-setup. These are Hi5 starter drafts. Rewrite each one in the member's voice, replace each [PROOF POINT] with ONE specific proof point the member gave you (or [ADD PROOF] if they have none), and add the pushback they hear most from this client type. Use only facts the member provided.

## 1. "I'll manage it myself."
1. **Behind it:** They want to save the fee and keep control.
2. **Ask first:** "Which parts of managing it would you most like to keep, and which would you rather skip?"
3. **Response:** That's a reasonable choice, and many owners do it well. The parts that tend to take the most time are marketing and showings, screening applicants consistently, handling repair calls, and keeping the paperwork in order. [PROOF POINT] If you'd like to manage it yourself, I can still help you get it rented and set up a simple checklist for the rest. Would you like that?
4. **Text version:** Totally respect that. Want a simple checklist for managing it yourself, even if I only help with getting it rented?
5. **Don't say:** "You'll regret doing it yourself." Anything that suggests they cannot handle it.

## 2. "I want to charge more rent than you suggest."
1. **Behind it:** They have costs to cover and want the highest return.
2. **Ask first:** "What number do you need, and what is that based on?"
3. **Response:** I understand, and I want you to get the most you reasonably can. What matters is what comparable places nearby are actually renting for, because a rent that's too high usually means more days empty. [PROOF POINT] Let's compare the numbers together, including what a month of vacancy would cost you. If the data supports your number, we can start there. Can we look at it together?
4. **Text version:** Let us look at what comparable places are renting for and what a month empty would cost. Want to do that together?
5. **Don't say:** Any guarantee about rent or how fast it will rent. "You'll never get that."

## 3. "Property managers charge too much."
1. **Behind it:** They are comparing the fee to the work and are unsure what it covers.
2. **Ask first:** "What would you want a manager to take off your plate?"
3. **Response:** That's fair, and the fee should be easy to understand. I'll show you exactly what's included, what isn't, and what the common costs are when a unit sits empty or turns over. [PROOF POINT] Then you can decide with the full picture, whether that's managing it yourself, hiring help, or something in between. Would you like me to put that on one page?
4. **Text version:** Fair question. Want a one-page breakdown of what is included and what it costs compared with doing it yourself?
5. **Don't say:** Anything negative about owners who self-manage. Hiding fees. Guarantees about tenants or income.

## 4. "My last tenant was a nightmare, so I do not trust anyone."
1. **Behind it:** They had a bad experience and fear repeating it.
2. **Ask first:** "What happened last time, and what would you want to be different?"
3. **Response:** I'm sorry that happened, and it makes sense that you're cautious. A clear process helps: written screening criteria applied the same way to every applicant, a thorough lease, and documentation from move-in day. [PROOF POINT] No process can promise a perfect outcome, but it can make problems less likely and easier to handle. Would you like to go through the process I'd follow?
4. **Text version:** I am sorry that happened. Want me to walk you through the screening and lease process I would use so it is more predictable this time?
5. **Don't say:** Promises about tenant quality. Screening on anything other than written, consistent criteria. Anything that sounds like legal advice about eviction or lease law.
