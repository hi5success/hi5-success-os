# Starter Objections: Commercial sales

Used by Stage 4 of /hi5-setup. These are Hi5 starter drafts. Rewrite each one in the member's voice, replace each [PROOF POINT] with ONE specific proof point the member gave you (or [ADD PROOF] if they have none), and add the pushback they hear most from this client type. Use only facts the member provided.

**Note for this client type:** For financing, tax, and legal questions, send the client to their lender, CPA, or attorney. Never promise returns or value.

## 1. "The price doesn't match the income."
1. **Behind it:** They believe the asking price is too high for the income the property produces.
2. **Ask first:** "What income and expenses are you basing that on?"
3. **Response:** That's the right question to ask. Let's walk through the property's income and expenses and the assumptions behind them, and compare them with similar properties. [PROOF POINT] If the numbers don't support the price, we can decide together whether to make an offer at a different price, ask for more information, or move on. Would you like to run the numbers together?
4. **Text version:** Good question. Want to go through the income and expenses together and compare with similar properties?
5. **Don't say:** Guarantees about value or return. Telling them what the property "will" earn. Pressure.

## 2. "We'll wait for interest rates to improve."
1. **Behind it:** They are worried about financing costs.
2. **Ask first:** "What financing terms would make this work for you?"
3. **Response:** That's a fair concern, and financing matters a great deal in commercial deals. I can't predict rates, but we can model the deal at today's terms and a few alternatives, so you can see what's workable. [PROOF POINT] A commercial lender can tell you what's available to you. Would you like me to set up that conversation?
4. **Text version:** Fair concern. Want to model the deal at today's terms and a couple of alternatives? A commercial lender can confirm what is available.
5. **Don't say:** Rate predictions. "Now is the time to buy." Quoting loan terms.

## 3. "We will deal directly with the seller or their broker."
1. **Behind it:** They want to save fees, or have a relationship with the seller.
2. **Ask first:** "What would you want help with, if anything?"
3. **Response:** That's your call, and it can work. A buyer's representative can help with pricing analysis, the letter of intent, due diligence, and negotiation. [PROOF POINT] I'll be clear about how I'm paid before we start, and we can decide together what help is worth it. Would you like me to explain what I'd handle?
4. **Text version:** Your call. I can help with pricing, the letter of intent, and due diligence. Want me to explain what I would handle and how I am paid?
5. **Don't say:** Disparaging the seller's broker. Hiding how you are paid. Pressure.

## 4. "We need to see the financials before we do anything."
1. **Behind it:** They need information before committing, and that is a reasonable position.
2. **Ask first:** "Which documents do you need first?"
3. **Response:** That's exactly the right approach. The usual path is to sign a confidentiality agreement if required, request the financials and leases, and review them before making any offer. [PROOF POINT] I'll help you build the document list and review it with you. Would you like me to request them?
4. **Text version:** Exactly right. Want me to request the financials and leases so we can review them before any offer?
5. **Don't say:** Pushing them to make an offer without information. Guessing at what the documents will show.

## 5. "Financing seems difficult."
1. **Behind it:** They worry about the lender requirements and approval.
2. **Ask first:** "What have you heard or experienced about financing?"
3. **Response:** That's a real consideration, because commercial lenders have their own requirements. The best step is to talk with a commercial lender early, so you know what they need and what you can expect. [PROOF POINT] I can introduce you and help you gather the documents. Would that help?
4. **Text version:** Real consideration. Want me to introduce you to a commercial lender early so you know what they need?
5. **Don't say:** Promising approval or terms. Telling them what they qualify for. Lending advice.
