# Starter Objections: Sellers

Used by Stage 4 of /hi5-setup. These are Hi5 starter drafts. Rewrite each one in the member's voice, replace each [PROOF POINT] with ONE specific proof point the member gave you (or [ADD PROOF] if they have none), and add the pushback they hear most from this client type. Use only facts the member provided.

## 1. "We'll wait for the market to get better."
1. **Behind it:** They hope for a better price, or fear selling at the wrong time.
2. **Ask first:** "What would you need to see change for it to feel like the right time?"
3. **Response:** That's a fair thing to weigh, and I don't want you to sell before you're ready. Nobody can time the market exactly, so I focus on what we can measure: what comparable homes are selling for today, how long they take, and what it would cost you to carry the home while you wait. [PROOF POINT] I can put those numbers side by side so you can see what waiting would actually have to deliver. Would that be useful before you decide?
4. **Text version:** Totally fair to want the right time. Want me to send a quick side-by-side of selling now versus waiting so you can see the numbers? No pressure.
5. **Don't say:** That the market is about to crash or jump. That they will lose money by waiting. Any prediction or guarantee about prices or rates.

## 2. "We're going to try selling it ourselves."
1. **Behind it:** They want to save the commission and feel confident they can handle it.
2. **Ask first:** "Which part of selling it yourself feels most doable, and which part worries you?"
3. **Response:** I respect that, and plenty of owners want to try. The parts that tend to surprise people are pricing it right, screening buyers, and handling the negotiation and paperwork. [PROOF POINT] If you'd like to give it a go, I'm happy to share a pricing review so you start from solid numbers, and the door stays open if you want help later. Can I send that over?
4. **Text version:** Happy to support whatever route you choose. Want a free pricing review to start from solid numbers? No strings.
5. **Don't say:** That for-sale-by-owner listings always fail. That they will leave money on the table. That they cannot do it without an agent.

## 3. "Another agent will do it for less."
1. **Behind it:** They are comparing price tags, and the value of the service is not clear yet.
2. **Ask first:** "Besides the fee, what matters most to you in how your home is sold?"
3. **Response:** That makes sense. Fees matter, and you should compare them. I'd just ask you to compare what each plan includes along with the price: the pricing strategy, the marketing, how offers are handled, and what happens if the first plan doesn't work. [PROOF POINT] I'm glad to walk you through exactly what you'd get so you can compare side by side. Would that help?
4. **Text version:** Fair to compare. Want me to send a one-page breakdown of exactly what is included so you can compare side by side?
5. **Don't say:** "You get what you pay for." Anything negative about the other agent. "I'll net you more." Do not cut your fee on the spot.

## 4. "We want to list higher than your number."
1. **Behind it:** They are attached to the home, need a certain amount to make their next move, or believe their improvements add more value.
2. **Ask first:** "What number do you need to make your next step work, and what makes you feel the home is worth that?"
3. **Response:** I can tell this home matters to you, and I want to understand your number. Here's how I think about it: pricing is a strategy, and buyers compare your home to what's nearby and what has sold. [PROOF POINT] Let's walk through the comparable homes together. If the data supports your number, we price there, and if it doesn't, we'll look at what the market is showing and agree on a plan to adjust. Does that feel fair?
4. **Text version:** Let's go through the comps together so we're looking at the same picture. Are you free tonight or tomorrow to do that?
5. **Don't say:** "Your home isn't worth that." "You'll never get that." "Overpriced homes never sell." Any guarantee about price or timing.

## 5. "We're just looking / not ready yet."
1. **Behind it:** They are not in a hurry, they fear pressure, or they are still gathering information.
2. **Ask first:** "What would 'ready' look like for you? Is there something you'd want to know first?"
3. **Response:** That's completely fine, and there's no pressure from me. Many owners find it helpful to understand their options well before they decide, so they're prepared when the time comes. [PROOF POINT] If it's useful, I can give you a quick read on where your home stands today and what you could do to prepare, with no obligation. I'll only check back in if you'd like me to.
4. **Text version:** No rush at all. If it helps, I can send a quick estimate of where your home stands today. No obligation.
5. **Don't say:** "You should act now before it's too late." Anything about prices or rates about to jump. False urgency, or repeated pushing after they said no.
