# Starter Objections: Luxury

Used by Stage 4 of /hi5-setup. These are Hi5 starter drafts. Rewrite each one in the member's voice, replace each [PROOF POINT] with ONE specific proof point the member gave you (or [ADD PROOF] if they have none), and add the pushback they hear most from this client type. Use only facts the member provided.

## 1. "Our home is unique, so the comps don't apply."
1. **Behind it:** They believe the home is one of a kind and the price should reflect that.
2. **Ask first:** "What makes your home different from anything that has sold nearby?"
3. **Response:** You're right that unique homes are harder to compare, and that's why the pricing strategy matters. We'll note everything that sets your home apart, and then use the nearest sales, active competition, and time on market to place it carefully. [PROOF POINT] Let's walk through it together and decide on a plan, including when we'd review the price. Would that work?
4. **Text version:** Great point. Want to walk through what sets your home apart and how we position it against the closest comparable sales?
5. **Don't say:** "Nothing is truly unique." Guarantees about price or timing. Dismissing the home's features.

## 2. "We want to keep it quiet and private."
1. **Behind it:** They want privacy and security, and want to avoid curious visitors.
2. **Ask first:** "What does privacy mean to you here: who sees it, who visits, or how it is advertised?"
3. **Response:** That's a reasonable priority, and it can be built into the plan. We can use pre-qualified showings, controlled access, and limited marketing, within the MLS and brokerage rules. [PROOF POINT] The tradeoff to understand is that limiting exposure also limits how many buyers see the home. Would you like me to lay out the options and tradeoffs?
4. **Text version:** Privacy can be part of the plan. Want me to outline the options and the tradeoffs?
5. **Don't say:** Suggesting ways around MLS or brokerage rules. Promising that a quiet listing will sell for more.

## 3. "Your fee should be lower on a higher-priced home."
1. **Behind it:** They see a large percentage of a large number.
2. **Ask first:** "What would you want the fee to cover?"
3. **Response:** That's a fair question to ask. A higher price usually means a larger marketing and service investment: professional media, targeted outreach, and more intensive buyer follow-up. [PROOF POINT] I'd rather show you exactly what's included and how it's measured, so you can judge the value. Can I walk you through the plan?
4. **Text version:** Fair question. Want me to show exactly what the marketing plan includes and how we measure it?
5. **Don't say:** Cutting your fee on the spot. "You get what you pay for." Anything negative about other agents.

## 4. "We want the biggest name brokerage or agent."
1. **Behind it:** They want reach and status.
2. **Ask first:** "What do you want a big name to do for you?"
3. **Response:** That's understandable, and reach matters for a home like yours. What I'd ask is how the person will actually market your home and be available to you, because that's what drives results. [PROOF POINT] I'll show you my specific plan for reaching qualified buyers and how I'll keep you updated. Would you like to see it?
4. **Text version:** Understandable. Want to see my specific plan for reaching buyers and how I will keep you updated?
5. **Don't say:** Disparaging any brokerage or agent. Vague claims about reach. Promising a result.

## 5. "We'll wait until the right buyer comes along."
1. **Behind it:** They are patient on price, and the carrying cost may not feel urgent.
2. **Ask first:** "How long are you comfortable waiting?"
3. **Response:** That's a legitimate strategy, and I respect your patience. It helps to put numbers on it: what it costs to carry the home each month, and how long comparable homes have taken. [PROOF POINT] We can agree on a plan with checkpoints, so you decide with facts at each step rather than by default. Would you like to set those checkpoints?
4. **Text version:** Fair strategy. Want to put numbers on the carrying cost and set a few check-in points so you can decide as we go?
5. **Don't say:** Predictions about when a buyer will appear. Pressure. "Overpriced homes never sell."
