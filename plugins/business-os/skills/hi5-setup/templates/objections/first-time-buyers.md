# Starter Objections: First-time buyers

Used by Stage 4 of /hi5-setup. These are Hi5 starter drafts. Rewrite each one in the member's voice, replace each [PROOF POINT] with ONE specific proof point the member gave you (or [ADD PROOF] if they have none), and add the pushback they hear most from this client type. Use only facts the member provided.

## 1. "We don't know if we're ready to buy."
1. **Behind it:** It is a big step, and they fear getting it wrong.
2. **Ask first:** "What would help you feel ready?"
3. **Response:** That's honest, and it's a big decision, so it's smart to take it seriously. A good first step is a no-obligation conversation to look at your budget, your goals, and your timeline, so you can see what's realistic. [PROOF POINT] You don't have to decide anything today, and I'll tell you plainly if waiting makes more sense. Want to start with that conversation?
4. **Text version:** Smart to take it seriously. Want to look at your budget and timeline together? No pressure, and no decision needed.
5. **Don't say:** "You're definitely ready." "Rents keep going up, so you should buy." Pressure of any kind.

## 2. "We'll keep renting until we have a bigger down payment."
1. **Behind it:** They believe a large down payment is required.
2. **Ask first:** "How much were you planning to put down, and where did that number come from?"
3. **Response:** Saving up is a smart goal. Many people are surprised that a large down payment isn't always required, and the options depend on your situation and where you live. [PROOF POINT] The best next step is a conversation with a lender about the programs available to you, so you know your real options and your real timeline. Want me to introduce you to one?
4. **Text version:** Saving up is smart! Many people are surprised by the options. Want me to connect you with a lender to see what is possible for you?
5. **Don't say:** Quoting down payment amounts, programs, or approval. "You only need X." Telling them what they qualify for.

## 3. "The process feels overwhelming."
1. **Behind it:** They fear unknown steps, paperwork, and jargon.
2. **Ask first:** "Which part feels most confusing right now?"
3. **Response:** That's very common, and you don't have to figure it out alone. I'll walk you through it one step at a time, explain every term in plain language, and tell you what's coming next so nothing surprises you. [PROOF POINT] We can start with a simple roadmap of the whole process. Would that help?
4. **Text version:** Totally normal. Want a simple step-by-step roadmap so you know what is coming?
5. **Don't say:** "It's easy." Jargon without explanation. Rushing them.

## 4. "We're afraid of making a mistake."
1. **Behind it:** The size of the purchase makes every choice feel risky.
2. **Ask first:** "What mistake worries you most?"
3. **Response:** That's a healthy worry, and it's why careful steps matter. We'll check the things that protect you: the price against recent sales, a thorough inspection, and a clear understanding of every cost before you commit. [PROOF POINT] You'll always have the facts before you decide, and the decision is always yours. Would you like me to show you what that looks like?
4. **Text version:** Healthy worry. Want me to show you the checks that protect you before you ever make an offer?
5. **Don't say:** "Nothing can go wrong." Guarantees about condition, value, or appreciation. Dismissing the worry.

## 5. "We don't want to be stretched too thin or hit with repair bills."
1. **Behind it:** They fear the monthly payment and surprise costs.
2. **Ask first:** "What monthly total feels comfortable once you include everything?"
3. **Response:** That's the right way to think about it. We'll look at the full monthly cost, not just the payment: taxes, insurance, and upkeep, and plan a cushion for repairs. [PROOF POINT] A thorough inspection will help you understand the home's condition before you commit. Want to build a realistic monthly budget together?
4. **Text version:** Right way to think about it. Want to build a realistic monthly budget that includes taxes, insurance, and upkeep?
5. **Don't say:** "You can afford more than you think." Telling them what to spend. Quoting costs you have not verified.
