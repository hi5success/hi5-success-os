# Quarterly Goals: Page Layout

This is the layout for each quarter on the member's **existing** Quarterly Goals page (`quarterly_goals_page_id`). Never create a new page or a new database. Keep older quarters on the page, newest first. Only change the section for the quarter being set or revised.

If the page body still contains only the line created by setup ("Created by /hi5-goals."), keep that line at the top, then add the quarter sections below it.

## Section for one quarter
Use a heading 2 named with the year and quarter and its dates, for example `2026 Q4 (Oct 1 to Dec 31)`. If the member plans by their own year, use their dates. Under it, write these parts in this order.

### Targets
A short table with one row per number, for example the quarter's GCI or revenue, take-home (only if their commission model or costs are saved), deals or clients, and the split by client type or offer.

### The math
The numbered steps from the industry file, each on its own line with the member's numbers. Label every assumption and its source ("from your numbers", "your estimate", or "I asked you").

### Priorities
Up to three priorities for the quarter, taken from the 90 day roadmap in their business plan. Always three or fewer if their blocker is taking on too much.

### Weekly plan
A table with 13 rows: week number, dates, the activity targets, and two or three actions.

### Check-in log
One line per weekly check-in, added over time: the week, the date, actuals against plan, the status (ahead, on track, or behind), and any adjustment. If the member told you where new agreements and closings came from, end the line with `Sources: Name count, Name count`. Leave a single line "No check-ins yet" until the first one.

### Review
One line, added by /hi5-bizreview, never by this skill: a link to the latest review on the Business Reviews page. When you revise a quarter, keep that line exactly as it is.

### Last updated
Today's date and what changed (set, revised, or check-in).

## Rules
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon, or a new sentence.
- Keep the wording plain. The member should be able to check every line.
- Do not remove a past quarter. Never overwrite a check-in log or the Review link line.
