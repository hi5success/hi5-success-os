# The Menu: What Can You Have?

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 2, right after the scan and before discovery. Members do not know what a dashboard can show, so suggest first, then run discovery only on what they pick. Keep it to one short message and one question.

## Build the menu from the scan
Offer only what the scan says is possible. Never offer a dashboard that needs an app the member does not have and could not plan around.
- **A) Command Center.** Always the first suggestion: the daily feed (today, email, tasks, and one summary card for each other dashboard they pick).
- **B) CRM.** If a CRM or GoHighLevel is connected, or the member uses one: leads, pipeline, clients by stage, follow-ups, and for real estate the potential revenue.
- **C) Content.** If the Content Planner (`content_planner_db_id`) has rows, or the member runs the content calendar: posting today, what needs their review, what is waiting on them, and what is stuck (from the Status Changed date and the Content SOP).
- **D) Money.** If Business Numbers or Quarterly Goals exist, or a finance connector is connected: targets against actual, cash in, take-home.
- **E) An industry dashboard.** Real estate members: Transactions (every file under contract, key dates, documents needed). Other industries: the one that fits from `industries/generic.md` (for example Jobs and quotes, Clients and deliverables, Recalls and reviews).
- **F) Something else.** A custom tracker the member describes.
Letters follow the order shown, so skip a letter rather than leave a gap. Say in the same message which connected apps each option uses, in a few words ("CRM uses GoHighLevel").

## How to ask
> "Here is what I can build for you, based on what I see: A) Command Center (today, email, tasks, and a card for each of the others) B) CRM (GoHighLevel) C) Content D) Money E) Transactions. Which do you want? You can pick several, and I recommend starting with A. I'll build each one as its own page, so you can add the others later."
Accept "all", a list of letters, or "just A". Never push more than they pick. If they only pick one that is not A, say once that the Command Center is where the daily feed lives, and offer it.

## After they pick
- Run discovery (`discovery.md`) only for the picked dashboards. Do not interview about a dashboard they did not pick.
- Every item on every picked dashboard still passes "If you saw this, what would you do?"
- Record the picks and the ones they passed on, on the Dashboards entries, so a later update can offer the passed ones once.
