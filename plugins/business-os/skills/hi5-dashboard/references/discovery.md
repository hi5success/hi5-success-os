# Discovery Interview

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Discovery comes first and widgets serve the answers. Scan first and skip anything the Master Profile, the personality profile, the Dashboards entry, and the connected apps already answer. Ask a few questions at a time, and show what you inferred as statements the member can correct.

## The questions
1. **First look.** What do you check first every morning? What do you open many times a day?
2. **Slipped through the cracks.** What has slipped through the cracks lately (a reply, a deadline, a document, a follow-up, an invoice)? What was it, and how did you find out?
3. **Weekly decisions.** What decisions do you make each week? What numbers would help you make them faster?
4. **Who and where.** Who will use this: just you, a team, or an assistant? Do they need to see the same things? Do you use it mostly on a phone or a computer?
5. **What you track now.** Where do you track things today (a spreadsheet, a notebook, a CRM, a board, your head)? Any dashboards you already have? Offer to review one they paste or describe.
6. **Anything you need to act on, not just see.** What would you want to do with one click (reply, make a task, mark something done, move something)?

## The filter (apply to every item)
For every item they mention, ask: **"If you saw this, what would you do?"**
- If the answer is a clear action (reply, call, send, approve, chase, decide, delegate), keep it and note the action. Make the action a button where the tools allow.
- If the answer is "nothing", or "it would be nice to know", drop it, or move it to a small "later" list. Say you are dropping it and why, in one line.
- If the answer depends on a number, write down the number and the threshold ("tell me when it is above X").

## What to capture (for the plan and the Dashboards entry)
- The first-look list, in order.
- The slips and the way each one should surface (a widget, an alert on a widget, or a date reminder).
- The weekly decisions and the numbers behind them.
- Each kept item with its action and its data source.
- Each dropped item with the reason.
- Who uses it and on what device.
- Existing dashboards reviewed, and what to keep or replace.

## Rules
- Never suggest a widget the member did not need. Presets in `industries/` are suggestions, offered after discovery, never forced.
- Never ask about an app or data the member said they do not use.
- Never ask for sensitive data. If the member offers some, use only what the widget needs.
- Keep the interview short. Stop when you can name every widget, its data source, and its action.
