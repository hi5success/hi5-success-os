# Starting Widgets for Any Industry

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 4. These are suggestions, offered after discovery, and never forced. Keep only what the member would act on. Every widget reads from the member's own apps, or from a Notion tracker the member approved.

## For almost everyone
- **Today:** appointments and calls from the calendar.
- **Email:** emails that need a reply, with drafted replies in the member's voice.
- **Tasks:** from Notion, and from the CRM if connected.
- **Important dates:** deadlines from the CRM or the calendar, or a Notion tracker.
- **Money snapshot:** the quarter's targets against actual from `/hi5-goals`, and cash in if a finance tool is connected.
- **Marketing snapshot:** the Marketing Hub and Content Planner at a glance.

## A service business (contractor, cleaner, consultant, repair)
- **Leads and quotes:** new leads, quotes sent and waiting, quotes to chase. From the CRM, or a Notion tracker (Quote, Client, Amount, Sent date, Status).
- **Jobs this week:** from the calendar, with what is needed for each.
- **Invoices:** unpaid and overdue, from the finance tool, with a "send a reminder" draft (sent only on click).

## An agency or a freelancer with clients
- **Clients:** one row per client with deliverables due, approvals waiting, and the last message. A Notion tracker (Client, Deliverable, Due date, Status, Owner), because the deliverables rarely live in an app.
- **Client email:** the email widget filtered by client (the member names the senders or domains).
- **Voices:** which voice to use for each client's drafts (from the Voice Library).
- **Invoices and retainers:** from the finance tool.

## A coach, teacher, or consultant
- **Sessions today:** from the calendar.
- **Clients needing a follow-up:** from the CRM, or a Notion tracker (Client, Program, Last session, Next step).
- **Program progress:** a Notion tracker with steps and status.
- **Payments:** from the finance tool.

## A local practice (dental, clinic, salon, studio)
- **Today's appointments:** from the calendar or the practice tool if connected.
- **Recalls and follow-ups due:** a Notion tracker unless the CRM holds them.
- **Reviews to answer:** a Notion tracker (Platform, Reviewer first name, Date, Status), and drafts in the member's voice. Reviews are answered on the platform, never auto-posted.
- **Never show health details on a dashboard.** Show names or initials and the next step only.

## An online store
- **Orders and issues:** from the commerce or finance connector if connected.
- **Customer email:** the email widget, filtered to support.
- **Inventory flags:** a Notion tracker unless a tool holds it.

## Rules
- Never invent a number or a status. An empty widget says what it needs.
- Do not show sensitive data (health, financial account numbers, private notes) unless the member asked for that widget, and then only what the widget needs.
