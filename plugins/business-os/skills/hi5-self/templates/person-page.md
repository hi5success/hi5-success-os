# Person Page Layout

Each person in the People database has one page with this body. Fill in only what is known and leave the rest as "Not shared".

> Profile for {{name}}, {{profile date}}. Source: {{Own answers | Estimated by [member name]}}. Re-run /hi5-self to update it.

### Role and reporting
Role, who they report to, human or AI, status.

### Behavioral style
The letters or blend, and two sentences on how they work, from their answers.

### How they communicate
How they explain things, how they like feedback, how they behave in a group.

### How they work and decide
Energy pattern and decision style, in plain words.

### Energy pattern (Human Design)
The type and authority if the person shared them, labeled as their own result or an approximation. Otherwise "Not shared". Never a birth date, time, or place.

### Notes
Anything the person asked the team to know about how to work with them, in their words. Nothing about health, family, or age.

The answers as they gave them (or the member's answers, if estimated), so the profile can be re-read later.
