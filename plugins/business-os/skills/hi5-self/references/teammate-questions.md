# Copy-Paste Question Set For A Teammate

Used by `teammate-profiles.md`. Give the member everything between the lines to send to the teammate. It uses work language, takes about 10 minutes, and has no wrong answers. Replace [MEMBER] and [NAME]. Do not add anything to it.

---
Hi [NAME], [MEMBER] is learning how each person on the team works best so messages, plans, and handoffs fit you better. These questions are about how you work. There are no wrong answers, and it is fine to skip anything you would rather not answer. Pick the letter that feels most like you (if two fit, write both and tell me which is stronger). You can add a note to any answer.

**How you work**
1. When you have a big work decision to make, you are most like:
A) I decide fast and adjust later  B) I talk it through with someone  C) I want everyone on board first  D) I research it before I decide
2. When something goes wrong at work, your first move is:
A) Fix it and move on  B) Tell someone so I can think out loud  C) Check that the people involved are okay  D) Find out exactly what happened first
3. In a meeting you are usually the one who:
A) Drives decisions  B) Keeps the energy up  C) Makes sure everyone is heard  D) Asks the careful questions
4. You like feedback to be:
A) Direct and blunt  B) Friendly and conversational  C) Gentle and thoughtful  D) Detailed, with examples
5. You do your best work when:
A) It is intense and fast  B) I am around people  C) The day is steady and predictable  D) I get long stretches of focus
6. When you start a new project you:
A) Jump in  B) Get excited and tell people  C) Make sure I have support and a plan  D) Map it all out first
7. When you are overloaded you tend to:
A) Push through  B) Talk to someone  C) Slow down and take care of myself and others  D) Step back and make a plan
8. What makes you lose motivation fastest:
A) Slow progress or micromanagement  B) Working alone with no feedback  C) Conflict or feeling unsupported  D) Moving fast without a clear plan

**How you like to receive information**
9. A message you will actually read is:
A) Two or three lines with the bottom line first  B) Short and warm, with the main point up front  C) Clear, friendly, and tells me what to expect next  D) Complete, with the details and the reasons
10. You prefer:
A) Bullets  B) A short story or example  C) Steps in order  D) A table or the data
11. When I ask you to do something, you want me to lead with:
A) What I need and by when  B) Why it matters and who it helps  C) What support you will have  D) The background and how I reached this
12. How direct should I be?
A) Very direct  B) Direct but friendly  C) Gentle, with room to talk  D) Direct and backed by facts
13. How much detail do you want by default?
A) Just the headline  B) Enough to get the idea  C) Enough to feel safe  D) Everything, so I can check it
14. When I send an update you do not need to act on, you want:
A) Nothing unless it changes  B) A quick note  C) A short check-in  D) A written summary

**Optional: energy and decisions**
15. When opportunities come, you:
A) Start your own  B) Respond to what shows up  C) Wait to be invited  D) Sample different things first
16. Your energy is:
A) Steady all day  B) In waves  C) Shaped by the people around me  D) Quiet and focused
17. Your best decisions come from:
A) A gut feeling right away  B) Sleeping on it  C) Talking it through  D) Waiting until it feels right
18. After a full day with people you feel:
A) Energized  B) Satisfied but ready to rest  C) Drained  D) It depends on the people

**Optional: Human Design.** Only if you want to share it. If you already know your Human Design type and authority, write them here. If you would like to look it up, a free chart tool will give you both. You do not need to send a birth date, time, or place, only the type and authority.

Thank you! Please send your answers back to [MEMBER].
---

## Scoring (for Claude, not sent)
- Questions 1 to 8: count letters. A is Driver, B is Influencer, C is Steady, D is Conscientious. Mixed answers: name the blend.
- Questions 9 to 14: read them directly into the Communication tips (length, format, lead with, directness, detail, updates).
- Questions 15 to 18: tendencies only (A initiator, B responder, C guide, D sampler). Always say these are approximate and never present them as a Human Design chart.
