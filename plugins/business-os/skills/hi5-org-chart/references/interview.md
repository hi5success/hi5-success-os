# First Run Interview

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 2. Scan first, skip anything the databases and the Master Profile already answered, and point to what you found ("I see Priya listed as a transaction coordinator, so I won't ask about her"). Ask a few questions at a time, never all at once.

## 1. The people
- Who is on the team (employees, contractors, partners, assistants, whoever the member counts)? One line each: name and what they do. The member is always on the chart.
- Does anyone work for the member part time or on a project basis only? (Mark them with the Status and a role that says so. Do not decide for the member who counts.)
- For each person without a profile, do not ask personality questions here. Mention /hi5-self once at the end.

## 2. Who reports to whom
- For each person: who do they report to? (Use the exact name as it appears in People.) The top person reports to no one.
- If two people could both be the manager, ask which one is the one to go to first. A person has one manager on the chart.

## 3. AI tools and assistants already in use
- Do you already use AI in your work in a way someone relies on (a Claude Project, a custom GPT, a scheduled task, an automation that drafts or sorts things)? Ask about each one: what it does and who looks after it.
- Every AI employee must have exactly one human manager: the person who checks its work and decides what it may do. If the member says "nobody", say that an AI employee with no owner is how things slip, ask who should own it, and let the member choose. If they will not choose, save it as Draft with no manager and flag it.
- An AI tool that only one person uses once in a while is not an AI employee. Offer to leave it off the chart and keep the chart for AI that has a recurring job. Let the member decide.

## 4. Jobs nobody owns
- Which recurring jobs in the business have no clear owner (for example "invoices", "social posting", "answering the main inbox")? List them as unowned roles. They show on the chart and in the highlights, and /hi5-bottleneck can help decide who or what should take them.

## 5. Confirm
Show the proposed rows as two tables (People, AI Employees), with every change labeled new or update. Wait for the member's OK. Write nothing before it.

For a new AI employee row, fill only what you know: Name, Role, Human manager, Status Draft, Created (today's date). Say: "The full job description for each AI employee comes from /hi5-bottleneck. For now the chart has its name, role, and manager."
