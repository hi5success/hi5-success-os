# Highlights

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 4. Highlights are prompts about gaps in the structure. They are not judgments of people. Show each on the chart and say it again in plain words in the chat. Show only the ones that apply, and show a short "nothing to flag" line when none do.

## 1. Humans with no AI help yet
A human with no AI employee under them. Say "[Name] has no AI employee yet." This is only a prompt. Many roles do not need one, so never say a person should have one. Offer /hi5-bottleneck for that person only if the member wants it.

## 2. Overloaded humans
From the Bottlenecks database (`bottlenecks_db_id`): add up the Hours per week of every open bottleneck (Status not Fixed or Dropped) for each Owner, matched to People by name. Show each human's total next to their name.
- **There is no standard threshold.** Ask the member once for the number of hours per week of recurring, slow work that they call "too much" for a person on this team, and save it in the member's own People page under Notes ("Overload hours per week: N"). A person at or above it is flagged.
- If there is no Bottlenecks database or no rows, say "Overload shows once /hi5-bottleneck has found some bottlenecks" and flag nothing. Never estimate overload from anything else.
- Say that the hours are what people reported, not a measurement.

## 3. AI employees with no manager or no review date
- No manager: the Human manager is blank, or does not match a person in People. Show it as an urgent flag. An AI employee with no human manager should not be Active.
- No review date: the Review date is blank. Suggest a date based on the review cadence it has (Monthly, Quarterly, or Twice a year). If it has none, ask the member which cadence to use. Never pick one for them.
- A review date in the past is also flagged ("Review overdue").

## 4. Roles nobody owns
Jobs from the first run interview that no one owns, and any open bottleneck with no Owner. List them in one box, with the source of each. Offer /hi5-bottleneck to decide who or what should take them.

## When the team is large enough for an AI Operations owner
Offer the AI Operations role once, never push it, when either holds: there are three or more AI employees, or there are eight or more people and at least one AI employee. These numbers are only a prompt, and the member decides. Describe the role in one line: a human whose job includes building, reviewing, and managing the AI employees. If the member declines, do not offer it again.
