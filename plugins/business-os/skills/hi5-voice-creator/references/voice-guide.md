# The Voice Guide

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 5. The guide is the heart of the voice. It is written from the samples and the interview, in plain words, and is short enough to use every time (aim for about 600 to 900 words). Use only what the samples and the member's answers show. Never invent a habit.

## Sections, in this order
1. **Summary.** Three sentences that capture how this person sounds and what a reader should feel.
2. **Tone.** The feeling (for example warm, direct, calm, funny), and what it is not.
3. **Sentence length and rhythm.** Short or long, how often they use fragments, how they use questions, how long a paragraph runs.
4. **Vocabulary.** Words and kinds of words they use (plain, technical, slang, regional), reading level, and the languages they write in and how they mix them.
5. **Greetings and sign-offs.** How they open and close in each channel, in their exact words.
6. **Signature phrases.** The phrases they say a lot, in their exact words. Rule: use sparingly, no more than one in a piece, and never in a way that reads like a catchphrase.
7. **Humor.** Kind, how much, and where they keep it out.
8. **Emoji and punctuation.** Which emoji, how many, where. Exclamation points, ellipses, capitals, lower case habits, and dashes. If the person uses em dashes as a habit, write it here and add: "Hi5 skills still avoid em dashes."
9. **Formality by audience.** One line each for clients, friends, social, and team.
10. **Openings and closings.** How they start and end an email, a text, a post, and a video or script.
11. **Storytelling.** Whether and how they tell stories, how long, and whether the point comes first. Rule: never invent a story for them. Use only stories the member gives you in the task.
12. **Slang and regional words.** The words, and how often.
13. **Never say.** Words, phrases, and moves they would never use, in their words, plus profanity (yes or no, and where).
14. **Do and don't.** Five to eight short lines each, in the form "Do ..." and "Don't ...".
15. **Channel notes.** One short paragraph each for email, text, social posts, video scripts, and long form (blog, newsletter, documents).
16. **Example lines.** Up to 8 short fragments in the person's own words that show the voice, each with a one-line note on why it is typical. Replace every phone number, address, email address, account number, and client or customer name with a placeholder such as [PHONE], [ADDRESS], or [CLIENT NAME]. Never include a full sample.
17. **Samples used.** A list of descriptions only: the kind (email, text, post, transcript), the approximate date, and where it came from. Never the raw text.

## Rules for writing the guide
- Use the person's name, or "they". Never guess a pronoun from a name.
- Every line must be backed by the samples or the interview. If something is a guess, leave it out or mark it "to confirm" for the test round.
- Describe style only. The guide never contains facts about the person's business, results, clients, or personal life.
- Keep the guide free of private details (see the scrub rule in `SKILL.md`).
- Version and date appear at the top: "Voice guide for [Name], version [N], [date]."
