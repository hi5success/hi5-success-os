# Hi5 Bizreview: Generic Flow (Any Industry)

Used by `SKILL.md`. Follow the core rules there: use only the pricing and cost model the member gave /hi5-bizplan, and show every step. Use the member's own words for what they sell ("clients", "customers", "projects", "sales").

## What to read from Business Numbers
- The goal and its type: `revenue_goal`, `take_home_goal`, or `client_goal`, plus `plan_period`
- Year to date: `deals_closed_ytd`, `revenue_ytd`
- The funnel and rates: `leads_received`, `agreements_signed`, `closed_transactions`, `avg_deal_value`
- The member's mix by offer: `client_mix` and `offers`
- The costs: `per_sale_fees`, `processing_fees`, `partner_fees`, `recurring_costs`, `outside_costs`, `fee_order_note`, and `take_home_note`
- `capacity_note`, `avg_days_to_close`
- The quarter's headline numbers: `quarter_clients_goal`, `quarter_revenue_goal`, `quarter_take_home_goal`

## Activity names
Use the names the weekly plan uses: leads or conversations, proposals or calls, clients won, and revenue that came in.

## Plan versus actual numbers
The activity numbers, clients won, and revenue. Show revenue actual against `quarter_revenue_goal` (or the plan to date), and take-home against `quarter_take_home_goal` if their costs are saved.

## By offer
If the member split the target by what they sell, review each offer: clients and revenue planned against actual, and name the one that is ahead and the one that is behind. If they did not split by offer, say so, and offer to review by offer if they give rough shares. If an offer has no breakdown in the check-ins, list a gap.

## Take-home (apply to the actual sales)
1. Get the revenue from each sale. Use the numbers in the check-ins or from the member. If only a total is known, divide by the sales and label it an average.
2. Use only what the member saved: `per_sale_fees`, `processing_fees`, `partner_fees`, `recurring_costs`, `outside_costs`, in the order they confirmed in `fee_order_note`. Never use a fee the member did not give, and never fill one in from what you know about a platform or processor. If a field is missing, ask once using the matching question (C1 to C5) in the /hi5-bizplan generic flow, save it, and continue. "None" and "not sure" count as answered, and a "not sure" line is not counted.
3. Show one sale at the average value as a short table in the confirmed order. Then multiply by the sales in the timeframe, subtract recurring costs prorated to the timeframe and the other costs they wanted counted, and show revenue and take-home side by side next to the plan.
4. If only `take_home_note` is saved, use that percent and label it an estimate from what they told you.
5. If no costs are saved at all, show revenue only and say: "Take-home needs your fees and costs. Run /hi5-bizplan to add them and I'll show both."

## Capacity
Compare actual clients with `capacity_note`. If the member is over capacity, say that wins are being held back by time, not by leads, and offer options: raise prices, add help, or narrow the offer. If they are under capacity and behind, the bottleneck is activity or conversion.

## Lead sources
Use `lead_sources_ranked` as the list. Use only what the member listed. Cost per sale needs spend per source. A source with no cost is shown as "no cost entered". For ads and paid sources, ask once for monthly spend.

## Rules for any-industry reviews
- A review describes numbers and activities only. Do not promise results, earnings, or timelines.
- Never quote a benchmark or an industry average as fact. Use the member's own numbers.
- Keep proof and earnings figures marked internal inside the review page only, never in anything public.
- Tax, legal, and financial questions are for their accountant, attorney, or advisor. A review is not that kind of advice.
