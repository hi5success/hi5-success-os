# Ranking

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 4.

## The score
For each bottleneck:
- **Hours per week** = time each time multiplied by how often, converted to a week. Show the arithmetic ("45 minutes x 4 a week = 3 hours"). Include the people involved if more than one person does it, and say so.
- **Pain** (1 to 5): how much it hurts. Ask the member. 1 is a mild annoyance, 5 is something that causes errors, lost clients, or stress.
- **Automatable** (1 to 5): how much of it a tool can do. You judge, show the reason in one line, and the member can change it. 1 means all judgment or relationships, 5 means the same steps every time. Never raise it because a tool is exciting.
- **Score** = hours per week x pain x automatable. Show the three numbers and the product.

## Show the top 3 to 5
A short table (Task, Owner, Hours per week, Pain, Automatable, Score) and one sentence of reason for each ("highest hours and it is the same steps every time"). Ties: the one with the lighter fix first. Never show more than five. Keep the rest in the Bottlenecks database as Found for later.

## Keep human
Tasks the member loves, tasks that need judgment, relationships, or a compliance decision are listed separately as Keep human, with the reason, whatever their score. A high score on a task like that is a prompt to ask whether a smaller part can be handed off (for example the prep, not the conversation).

## Estimated hours saved
For each recommended fix, show a range:
- **Low** = the hours per week of the steps the fix fully handles, minus the time a human will still spend reviewing or approving.
- **High** = the low number plus the hours of the steps the fix partly handles.
- Use the member's own numbers for each step. If a step has no number, ask or leave it out and say so.
- Label it clearly: "Estimate, not a promise." Never use a standard percentage, a benchmark, or a number from memory.
- Ask the member to note how long the task takes after the fix for two weeks, and record it as Actual hours saved on the next run.
