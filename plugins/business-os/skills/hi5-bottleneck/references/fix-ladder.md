# The Fix Ladder

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 5. Stop at the first rung that works, and say why the lighter rungs do not.

## a. Remove or simplify the step
Ask: does this task need to exist at all? Can it be shorter, done less often, batched, or handled by a template, a checklist, or a rule? Often the best fix costs nothing. Offer it first.

## b. An existing Hi5 skill or dashboard widget (always check first)
Read `hi5-next/references/path.md` (the live skills), the member's Dashboards entry (the widgets they already have), and what is installed. Match the task to what already exists, for example:
- Drafting emails, newsletters, posts, scripts, funnels, and pages: the writing skills in Marketing OS and Content OS.
- Replying to email, a follow-up queue, today's schedule, important dates, tasks, a pipeline or deal tracker: the /hi5-dashboard widgets.
- Plans, goals, check-ins, and reviews: /hi5-bizplan, /hi5-goals, /hi5-bizreview.
- Writing in a person's voice: /hi5-voice-creator.
- Real estate day planning, files, sphere, prospecting, buyers, ads, rentals, and neighborhoods: the /hi5-re- skills.
If one covers it, point to it, show how to run it for this task, and stop. Do not build anything new.

## c. Platform automation
If the member's own platform can do it (a GoHighLevel workflow, a calendar rule, an email rule, a form's notification, a scheduler's reminders), give exact step by step instructions from `platform-instructions.md`. Never change their platform from here. Any automation that sends a message must respect consent, do-not-contact, and the Hi5 messaging rules, and must never send in bulk without the member's own consent process.

## d. AI help
If it needs judgment or writing but is a single job: a prompt to paste, a skill (saved with the save skill card plus a backup file), or a scheduled task that does a read only or draft only job on a schedule. Build it with `quick-win.md` after the member's OK. Drafts are never sent without the member's approval.

## e. An AI employee
Only when the work is a role with several recurring duties that share inputs, tools, and one human owner (as a rule of thumb, three or more recurring duties). Follow `ai-employees.md`. If it is really one or two jobs, use rung d.

## f. Keep it human
Say why: it needs judgment, it is a relationship, it is a compliance decision, or the member loves it. Offer to hand off only the prep or the paperwork around it.

## For each recommendation, show
The task, the rung chosen, why the lighter rungs do not work, what it will take (who builds it, how long, what the member must approve), and the estimated hours saved as a range (labeled as an estimate), and what could go wrong.
