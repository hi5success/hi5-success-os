# Platform Automation Instructions

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 6 and `fix-ladder.md` rung c. This skill never changes GoHighLevel or any other platform. It gives instructions the member follows.

## Format for every automation
Write numbered steps the member can follow. Screens and menu names differ between platforms and versions, so say "look for" and never claim exact names. Ask for a screenshot if their screen does not match. For each automation, give:
1. **Goal.** One sentence.
2. **Trigger.** What starts it (a form submitted, a stage change, a tag added, a date reached, an appointment booked).
3. **Filters or conditions.** Who it applies to and who it must skip.
4. **Actions.** In order, with the exact wording for any message (written in the member's voice, with the disclosure line where email requires it).
5. **Waits and branches.** Any delay, and what happens if the person replies or books.
6. **Test.** How to test it on the member's own contact first, and what to check.
7. **Turn it off.** How to stop it.
8. **What to watch.** What could go wrong, and when to review it.

## Rules for any automation that sends a message
- Texts only to people with documented consent, and never to anyone marked do-not-disturb or who replied STOP. If consent is unknown, use email.
- Send inside the allowed hours for the person's time zone (8am to 9pm for texts).
- Include the disclosure line and the unsubscribe or opt-out wording where the channel requires it.
- Never write an automation that sends in bulk to a list the member has not consented to. Real estate: never skip trace, never text a scrubbed number without consent, and follow Fair Housing, so never filter or target by who someone is.
- Say that the member owns turning the automation on, and that they should test it on themselves first.

## GoHighLevel
GoHighLevel is the first platform for most members. Write the workflow as trigger, filters, actions, waits, and test, using the language of workflows ("look for Automation or Workflows"). Mention that the Real Estate GHL Snapshot, if they use it, may already include a similar workflow, and tell them to check before building a second one.

## Other platforms
Use the same format with their own words for triggers and actions. If you are not sure how a platform works, say so and ask the member to paste what they see. Never guess a feature exists.
