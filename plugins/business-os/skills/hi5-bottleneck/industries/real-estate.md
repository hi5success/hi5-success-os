# Bottleneck Examples for Real Estate

Used by `SKILL.md`. These are suggestions only, offered after the interview, and never assumed. Real estate rules always apply: Fair Housing (never rank, filter, or describe people by who they are), texting consent and do-not-contact checks, drafts only, compensation set by agreement and never a standard rate, and browser steps only on the member's own MLS login in the places Real Estate OS already allows.

## Often slow, and what already exists (check these first)
- **Morning planning, who to call, week ahead, inbox triage:** /hi5-re-daily and the dashboard email and today widgets.
- **Follow-up that slips:** the Follow-Up Queue widget in /hi5-dashboard, /hi5-re-sphere for past clients, a GoHighLevel workflow for new-lead response (instructions only).
- **Listing launch content and just-listed or just-sold posts:** /hi5-re-listing-launch.
- **Listing appointment prep and CMAs:** /hi5-re-listing-appt.
- **Contract to close:** /hi5-re-transaction, and the transaction tracker widget in /hi5-dashboard.
- **Seller and buyer updates:** /hi5-re-seller-updates and /hi5-re-buyer.
- **Prospecting expireds, FSBOs, and a farm:** /hi5-re-prospect (never automatic, scrub confirmation before any call or text).
- **Reviews and referrals:** a plain request through /hi5-re-sphere, and a GoHighLevel review-request workflow (instructions only, consent first).
- **Ad copy and lead follow-up:** /hi5-re-ads.

## Possible AI employees (only for several recurring duties, with one human manager)
- **Transaction coordinator assistant:** reads the executed contract, keeps the file checklist and key dates, drafts weekly client updates and reminders. Human manager: the agent or the coordinator. Never sends, never changes dates.
- **Inbox and lead triage assistant:** reads the inbox read only, sorts what needs a reply, drafts replies in the member's voice, and builds the day's follow-up list. Human manager: the agent.
- **Listing marketing assistant:** drafts remarks, captions, a content calendar, and a graphics brief for each new listing, with a Fair Housing scan. Human manager: the agent or the marketing lead.
- **Follow-up and sphere assistant:** drafts the next touch for each person who needs one, with the texting consent gate on every text. Human manager: the agent.

## Often best kept human
Listing and buyer consultations, price conversations, negotiation, offer decisions, client relationships, and anything about compensation or a legal or compliance question.
