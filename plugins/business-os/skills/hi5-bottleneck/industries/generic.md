# Bottleneck Examples for Any Business

Used by `SKILL.md`. These are suggestions only, offered after the interview, and never assumed. Check what Hi5 already does before building anything.

## Often slow, and what already exists (check these first)
- **Email that needs a reply:** the email widget in /hi5-dashboard, with drafts in your voice.
- **Writing newsletters, emails, posts, pages, case studies:** the writing skills in Marketing OS and Content OS.
- **Following up with leads, quotes, or past customers:** the Follow-Up Queue widget in /hi5-dashboard.
- **Planning, goals, and reviews:** /hi5-bizplan, /hi5-goals, /hi5-bizreview.
- **Tasks and important dates:** the tasks and important dates widgets.
- **Scheduling, reminders, and intake forms:** your platform's own automation (instructions only).
- **Invoices and payment reminders:** your finance tool's own reminders, or a draft reminder you send yourself.

## Possible AI employees (only for several recurring duties, with one human manager)
- **Inbox and follow-up assistant:** sorts the inbox, drafts replies, and builds the follow-up list.
- **Content assistant:** plans and drafts a week of content from your ideas, in your voice.
- **Operations assistant:** keeps checklists, reminders, and a weekly summary of what is stuck.
- **Client onboarding assistant:** prepares the welcome material and the checklist for each new client.
Each drafts and never sends, publishes, spends, or changes records on its own.

## Often best kept human
Sales conversations, client relationships, pricing decisions, hiring and people decisions, and anything legal, medical, financial, or sensitive.
