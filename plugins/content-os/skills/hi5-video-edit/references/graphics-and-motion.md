# Graphics and Motion

CapCut is the editor. Graphics and motion are made outside CapCut and then placed in the CapCut project with the capcut tools.

## Who does what
- **Made by Claude:** anything that has to be designed because it does not exist yet: infographics, charts and number reveals, animated maps (a route, an area outline, "10 minutes from downtown"), title cards, animated lower thirds, explainer diagrams, logo stings.
- **CapCut (through the bridge):** everything built from footage that already exists: cuts and trims, B-roll placement, captions and subtitles, simple text, transitions, music and sound, zooms and keyframes, and the final assembly, including placing the graphics Claude made.

## How Claude makes a graphic, in this order
1. **Claude Motion**, if the member has it (a beta on some Claude plans, not every member yet). It exports an MP4. Ask once whether they have it, and remember the answer in Video Preferences.
2. **Another way to make motion**, if Claude Motion is not available: an animated graphic Claude can build and render with what this computer already has. Do not install a program for it.
3. **A still image** with a slow keyframe zoom (capcut_add_image plus capcut_add_keyframe). Always works.

Images for a still or a background can also come from ChatGPT in the member's browser (it can save into the shared cloud folder). Higgsfield or Runway are optional paid extras: use them only if the member has them connected and asks. Remotion exists but is tabled for now: do not suggest or install it.

## Placing it
- Save each graphic into the project's `B-Roll` folder (or `B-Roll/Made by Claude` if the member uses that), named so it sorts: `G01 - market chart.mp4`, `G02 - area map.png`.
- Match the canvas: 16:9 for a long-form, 9:16 for a short. Keep a graphic 3 to 8 seconds unless the Edit notes say otherwise.
- Add it with capcut_add_video (or capcut_add_image) on its own overlay track at the time in the Edit notes, above the main footage and below captions.
- Follow the default rules in video-defaults.md: never over a face, and never covering captions.

## Facts on graphics
Every number, date, price, and map label comes from the script, the row, or the member, or it gets a [confirm: ...] placeholder. The pre-review check covers graphics and on-screen text too: compliance and disclosure, no income or results claims, and on real estate content Fair Housing (a map shows places and drive times, never who lives somewhere, and never rates schools or safety).
