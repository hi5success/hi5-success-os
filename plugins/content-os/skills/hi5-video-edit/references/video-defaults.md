# Video Defaults and Preferences

## Order of what wins
1. The hard rules (compliance, Fair Housing, no invented facts).
2. The member's brand: the brand's Style Guide and the Brand folder (fonts, colors, logo, cover examples).
3. The member's **Video Preferences** (below).
4. The row's Edit notes and the Edit Log.
5. These built-in defaults.
For a team editor, all of these are the client's, never another client's.

## Video Preferences (the member's standing preferences)
A plain file at `Content/Brand/Video Preferences.md` in the member's root (for a team, in that client's Brand folder). The member lists how they always want videos edited, one line each, for example "captions yellow, bottom third", "no music on talking-head videos", "always end on the logo card", "I have Claude Motion: yes". Read it before every edit, and say in one line which preferences you applied.
- If it does not exist, edit with the defaults, and at the end ask once: "Want me to save any of these choices as your standing video preferences?" Create the file only with their OK.
- When the member corrects an edit and says it applies every time, offer to add it as one line. One line per lesson. Never remove a line without their OK.
- This file is for editing style only, never passwords, logins, or personal information.

## Built-in defaults
- **Never put graphics, text, captions, stickers, or a logo over the speaker's face or anyone else's face.** Move it, shrink it, or cut to a full-screen graphic instead.
- Captions never cover on-screen text that matters (a slide, a price, a sign). Move them up or skip that line.
- Safe margins: keep text inside the middle 80 percent of the frame. On 9:16, keep it out of the bottom fifth and the right edge, where the app's buttons sit.
- Music sits well under the voice; voice is always clear. No music on a video the member said should have none.
- Cut long silences, false starts, and repeated takes, but never cut words that change the meaning.
- No added effects, transitions, or stickers the type does not call for. Clean beats busy.
- Keep the member's colors and fonts. If none are saved, use plain white text with a soft shadow.
- Don't change color or quality on screen recordings.
