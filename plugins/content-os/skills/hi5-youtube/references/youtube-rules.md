# YouTube Package Rules

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Everything is drawn from the transcript, the row's notes, the Style Guide, and the Edit Log.

## Title
- Front-load the topic or keyword people search for, and say what the viewer gets. Plain and specific beats clever.
- Never promise a result the video does not deliver, and never use a claim the member did not make. No income or results claims.
- Give three options and mark the recommended one.

## Description
- The first two lines carry the hook and the main keyword, because they show before "more".
- Then a short summary in the member's voice, the chapters, the links the member gave, and the member's `disclosure_line_short` (video descriptions use the short line) plus any required notices.
- Plain text only. No hidden filler, no keyword lists pasted at the bottom.

## Chapters
- Built from the timestamped transcript. The first chapter starts at 00:00, there are at least three chapters, and each is at least 10 seconds long (YouTube generally requires this for chapters to show). Check against the current YouTube help if unsure.
- Short, plain chapter names that match what is said.

## Tags
- A few relevant tags only, drawn from the title, the topic, and the research notes. Do not stuff tags.

## SEO and AEO
Use the topic's search wording from the research notes on the row. Answer the viewer's main question clearly in the description's first lines so search and AI answers can quote it.

## Notes for Social
For each clip or short from this video, add to its YouTube notes: a title idea, a few tags, and the link to this long-form (add the link only when the video is public or scheduled).

## Never
Never invent facts, timestamps, or claims. Never write captions for short-form (Social does that).
